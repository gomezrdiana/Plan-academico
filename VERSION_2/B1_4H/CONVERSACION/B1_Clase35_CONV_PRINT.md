## ANEXO — VERBATIM DEL LIBRO + PLAN B + NOTA INTERNA

### M3 — Deduction (Guide 1 p.23; Examples p.24; Exercises p.25)
Cita p.23: *"we use verbs of deduction to express a supposition... The verb depends on the certainty the speaker feels: Very certain -> Must; Partially certain -> Might / May / Could; Very certain of the opposite -> Can't / Must not."* + *"It is very common to use deduction with the verb 'to be'... 'He must be playing soccer.' / 'He must have arrived already.'"*
Ejemplos verbatim p.24: *"Horst is late. He must be sick."* · *"Mike is here. He can't be sick."* · *"Sam must have arrived last night. His car is here."* · *"The boys can't be playing soccer. I have their ball."*

### M4 — The Third Conditional (Straight) (Guide 1 p.29; Examples p.30; Exercises p.31)
Cita p.29: *"refers to something that did (or did not) happen in the past... Clause with 'if': HAD + PARTICIPLE / Conditional clause: WOULD HAVE + PARTICIPLE. 'If I had studied… I would have passed the exam.'... The probability of studying is now 0%, because past events cannot be changed."*
Ejemplos verbatim p.30: *"If you had woken up on time, we wouldn't have been late."* · *"If Sarah had said good morning, I would have talked to her."* · *"If you had found your brother, what would you have said?"* · *"If we had asked the teacher, would he have told us the answer?"*
