## BLOQUE 1 — APERTURA (20')

**Objective: reactivate degrees/so-too-enough and gerunds-as-subjects, then aim at ownership and feelings.**

**Puntualidad: coordinación registra la hora de llegada; foto de inicio opcional.**

1. **Recuperacion (10', oral, DE PIE, cadena, sin cuaderno).** Disparadores (Cl 29 y Cl 25); errores anonimos al board; 1 min re-produccion:
   - "Judge a level: one 'enough', one 'too', one 'so' about your work." (Cl 29)
   - "Fix it: 'The plan is enough good.'" (Cl 29)
   - "Gerund as subject: 'Planning is...', 'Learning is...' — one true work line." (Cl 25)
   - "Fix it: 'Plan is important for my job.' (subject as -ing)" (Cl 25)
   - "Chain: 'so ___' + 'Working ___ is ___.'" (Cl 29/25)
2. **Chequeo de portafolio con lista en mano (3').** Ya viste los videos antes de entrar; el chequeo nombre por nombre es SOLO tuyo (Gramatica solo recuerda el video). Marcas ENVIO o NO ENVIO y al que envio le dices en voz alta **UNA cosa por mejorar, en 5 segundos, con el error real**. El video que no abre con sus 3 frases de LISTEN FIRST cuenta como incompleto. Al que no envio: *"You send the missing video TODAY, together with today's. Two videos, same day. The list goes to coordination today."*
3. **Frase del Dia (4').** Board antes; 2 lecturas + coro + 1 solo; 3+ inserciones. Señala: *"'Whose is this?' - 'It's MINE.' We give each their own. And '-ING' describes the thing, '-ED' describes me: the work is tiring, I am tired. Naming that honestly is Justice, day two."*
4. **Lectura del PASE de Gramatica, en voz alta (3').** Lees los 3 errores escritos del PASE que te dejo Gramatica en su ultima sesion: *"These came out of your writing in Grammar. Now they have to survive out loud."* Los gaps (a)-(b) del PASE son tus blancos de hoy.

> [VIRTUD] Board: "JUSTICE — DAY 2". *"Justice gives to each what is theirs — the credit, the tool, the space — and it names feelings truthfully, without exaggeration. Owning fairly and feeling honestly is just."*

## BLOQUE 2 — WHOSE IS IT? / TIRING vs TIRED (40')

**Objective: everyone claims ownership and names a real work feeling, fast, across both structures.**

1. Meta en 1 linea (arriba). **Avance de proyecto hoy: una linea para MY STORY — "this progress is mine: I earned it, and the journey was exciting, not exhausting."**
2. Board — el molde oral (Gramatica lo sella en papel en su sesion con M39 + M41; aqui NO se re-explica la regla):
```
OWNERSHIP & FEELINGS AT WORK
OWNERSHIP: "Whose laptop is this?" - "It's mine." / "It's hers." / "It's theirs."
           "Whose idea was better?" - "Mine was."
FEELINGS:  "-ING" = the thing causes it: "The report is tiring."
           "-ED"  = I feel it:           "I am tired."
           "The meeting was boring; I was bored."  "The news is exciting; I'm excited."
(mine/yours/his/hers/ours/theirs · whose...? · -ing thing vs -ed person)
```
3. Modela 45 seg: reclama algo tuyo del trabajo ("This project is mine") + nombra un sentimiento real ("The deadline was stressful, but the result is exciting").
4. Preparacion en parejas (6'): cada uno elige 2 objetos/tareas reales (¿de quien es?) y 2 situaciones reales (¿tiring o tired? ¿interesting o interested?).
5. Ejecucion rapida (14'): en parejas, A pregunta "Whose ___ is this?" y B responde con pronombre; luego B nombra una situacion y A reacciona con la forma correcta (-ing thing / -ed person). Cambio de pareja a mitad.
6. Producto verificable (10'): vuelta completa — cada estudiante dice al grupo UNA frase de posesion Y una de sentimiento (20 seg). Tu cuentas moldes limpios (mine vs my / tiring vs tired) en el board.
7. Feedback: logro primero, forma despues. Errores 2+ parejas -> board anonimo. Nombres a tu cuaderno.

## BLOQUE 3 — THE PROJECT HANDOVER (35')

**Objective: hold a realistic end-of-project handover — claim items and report honestly how the work felt.**

1. Board: `PROJECT HANDOVER — "WHOSE IS THIS? HOW WAS IT?" — 3 ROTATIONS`
2. Escenario profesional realista (solo tablero y sala): cierre de un proyecto/evento; el equipo reparte materiales ("¿de quien es esto?") y cada quien reporta como fue la experiencia. **1 guest = quien coordina el cierre** (*"Whose files are these? — They're mine. Whose laptop is on the desk? — It's hers. The last week was exhausting and I'm a bit tired, but the launch was exciting and everyone is proud."*); otro estudiante rota como quien reclama y responde (*"That folder is mine. The process was frustrating, but I'm satisfied now."*); el resto = **OBSERVERS ACTIVOS** (2 errores, 1 acierto, 1 pregunta). Tu = COACH desde afuera — NUNCA juegas guest.
3. 3 rotaciones (~9'). Debrief por TIPOS (anonimo): moldes limpios vs "it's my" (falta pronombre) / "I am boring" (por bored) / "the meeting was tired".
4. **Cierre JUSTICIA (3'):** *"Justice day two — name one thing at work that is truly yours, and one honest feeling about this week (the -ed word)."* — 2 respuestas maximo.
