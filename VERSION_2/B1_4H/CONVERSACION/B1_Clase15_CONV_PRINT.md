## BLOQUE 2 — EL PASADO QUE ESTABA EN MOVIMIENTO (40')

**Objective: by the end, every one of you paints a real moment of your past for a partner — five sentences about what you and the people around you were doing — with WAS and WERE in the right place.**

1. **Avance de proyecto hoy: MY STORY, MY GOALS — pieza "WHERE I WAS IN MY LIFE" (1').** Di: *"One piece every class. Today, a picture: where you were living, what you were doing, who was with you — at one moment of your story."*
2. **Tablero, solo uso ORAL (3') — la tabla y el contraste en papel los sella Gramatica Cl 15:**
```
THE PAST THAT WAS STILL MOVING  (A2 Book M12 p.100-101 · A1 Book M32 p.361)
 POINT (past simple):  "I played yesterday."          -> it happened, it finished
 LINE  (past continuous): "I was playing for three hours." -> it was in progress
 I / he / she / it  + WAS  + verb-ING     "I was living in Bucaramanga in 2019."
 you / we / they    + WERE + verb-ING     "We were working all morning."
 "Eliana was studying for her exams last week."
 QUESTION:  "What were you doing at 9:00?"   "Where was Joe working last year?"
 NEGATIVE:  "I wasn't driving."  "They weren't working."
NEVER "I was work" · NEVER "they was working" · NEVER "I working at nine"
```
3. **Modelas 60 seg (2').** Pintas un momento real de tu vida (*"In 2019 I was living in a small apartment. I was working in a call center, and I was studying English at night. My sister was living with me; she was looking for a job."*). Señalas WAS / WERE y el -ING con el dedo; NO explicas.
4. **Freeze frame, DE PIE (7').** Circulo. Tu dices un momento del pasado (*"Yesterday at 7:00 PM"*). Cada estudiante dice que estaba haciendo (*"I was cooking."*); el de al lado lo **reporta en tercera persona** antes de decir el suyo (*"She was cooking. I was driving home."*). Cambias el momento cada vuelta (*"last weekend at noon" · "this morning at six" · "in 2020"*). Si alguien dice "she were", el grupo responde en coro: *"One person — WAS."* Velocidad creciente. El drill NO cierra el bloque.
5. **Meta del bloque — preparacion en parejas (5', notas minimas en su propio papel).** *"By the end, each of you paints one real moment of your past: five sentences — what you were doing, what two other people were doing, and one thing that was NOT happening."*
6. **Ejecucion en parejas, 2 rondas (11').** A pinta su momento; B escucha y **hace 2 preguntas con was / were** (*"What were your colleagues doing? Were you working alone?"*). Cambian. Cambio de pareja, segunda ronda sin papel y mas rapida. Numero impar = un trio.
7. **Pieza de proyecto, en voz (5').** Cada uno dice a una pareja nueva su **"WHERE I WAS IN MY LIFE"**: un año clave, donde estaba viviendo, que estaba haciendo, quien estaba con el. La pareja repite un dato y hace UNA pregunta. Las lineas se anotan en su propio papel para el proyecto.
8. **Producto + feedback (6').** 3-4 estudiantes dicen su pieza al grupo. Feedback primero sobre el LOGRO (*"could we see the picture?"*), despues sobre la forma. Errores que aparezcan en 2+ parejas al tablero ANONIMOS, coro 2x; nombres y citas a tu cuaderno.

## BLOQUE 3 — SIMULACION: SHIFT HANDOVER — "WHAT WAS HAPPENING ON YOUR SHIFT?" (35')

**Objective: hand over a real work shift to a colleague — what each person and each area was doing — with WAS and WERE under pressure, and nobody left out.**

1. **Tablero (1'):** `SHIFT HANDOVER — 1 OUTGOING SUPERVISOR (GUEST) — 1 INCOMING SUPERVISOR — EVERYONE ELSE OBSERVES`
2. **Escenario profesional realista (solo tablero y sala), 2'.** Cambio de turno en una bodega, un hospital, un hotel o un call center: el supervisor que sale le cuenta al que entra como estuvo el turno, en 5 minutos, de pie, como se hace en cualquier operacion. **1 estudiante = guest / supervisor que sale:** dice que estaba pasando en cada area y que estaba haciendo cada persona por rol (*"At six the drivers were loading the trucks. The new assistant was checking the orders. Machine two was running slow all night. I was answering the client's calls."*), y una cosa que NO estaba pasando (*"Nobody was working in area C."*). **1 estudiante rota como supervisor que entra:** pregunta con was / were (*"What was the team doing at midnight? Was machine two running at five? Were the drivers waiting?"*). **TU eres COACH desde afuera — NUNCA eres ninguno de los dos.** Si se congelan 15 seg, sueltas UN prompt (*"Tell him what the drivers were doing"*) y te retiras.
3. **Minimos por rotacion:** 6 frases con was / were + -ing (al menos 3 sujetos distintos: I, una persona, un grupo) · 1 negativo (wasn't / weren't) · 3 preguntas del que entra · ninguna area ni persona del turno queda sin nombrar.
4. **OBSERVERS ACTIVOS con ficha en su propio papel (la dibujan en 30 seg):** `WAS (count)` · `WERE (count)` · `"-ING" MISSING / "THEY WAS"` (el error literal) · `ANYONE LEFT OUT? (yes / no)`. Al cerrar cada rotacion cantan: *"four WAS, three WERE, one 'they was', nobody left out."*
5. **4 rotaciones (~6' cada una)**, guest y entrante nuevos; el lugar cambia (bodega, hospital, recepcion de hotel, call center). Entre rotaciones, 45 seg de cadena rapida de pie: *"I was · you were · she was · they were — working."*
6. **Debrief (4').** Los observers reportan TIPOS y conteos, nunca personas (*"-ING missing twice", "area C was left out in rotation three"*). Correcciones al tablero, coro 2x. Intervienes solo si un error sale 3 veces.
