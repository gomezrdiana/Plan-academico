2. **Tablero, solo uso ORAL (3') — la etiqueta en papel la sello Gramatica Cl 20 a las 8:00:**
```
THE WHOLE PAST — 5 forms, one story, no present leak
(1) THE SCENE         was / were + -ing  (+ an exact time)
(2) THE TURN          when + past simple
```
