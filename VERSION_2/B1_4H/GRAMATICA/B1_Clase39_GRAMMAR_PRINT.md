## BLOQUE 2 — PERFECT TENSES ON PAPER (40') — DE PIE ~90%

**Objective: everyone builds present, past and future perfect (+ continuous) and uses already/yet correctly.**

1. Board — cita del libro (M9):
```

           for/since/already/yet/just: "I've lived here SINCE 2014."
PAST PERF: had + participle -> before another past
           "I had already left when she called."
FUT PERF : will have + participle -> done by a deadline
           "I will have finished by 5pm."
PERF CONT: has/have/had/will have + been + V-ing -> duration
           "I have been working all day."
ALREADY -> affirmative | YET -> negative/question, at the end
```
2. Señala: *"Same participle you used for reported speech. Here it rides on HAVE, HAD, or WILL HAVE."*
3. **Conveyor chain (10', DE PIE, circulo):** tu lanzas un contexto ("the audit — still open"); est. 1 lo pone en present perfect (with since/for), 2 en past perfect (before a past), 3 en future perfect (by a deadline), 4 en perfect continuous (duration). Velocidad creciente.

## ANEXO — VERBATIM DEL LIBRO + PLAN B (no leer literal en clase)

**M9 (p.97-116) — reglas y ejemplos citados verbatim:**
- **Present perfect (have/has + participio):** periodo abierto / no completo / muy reciente; for/since/already/yet/just. "I have lived here since 2014." "I have just finished." "Have you ever tried it?"
- **Past perfect (had + participio):** accion antes de otra pasada. "I had already left when she called."
- **Future perfect (will have + participio):** completado para un limite de tiempo. "I will have finished by 5pm."
- **Perfect continuous (has/have/had/will have + been + V-ing):** duracion de una accion en curso. "I have been working all day."
- **already / yet:** ALREADY -> afirmativo; YET -> negativo/pregunta, al final. "I haven't finished YET." "Have you sent it YET?"

**PLAN B por bloque:**
- B1: si flojean los modales perfectos, drilea 4 participios en el aire antes de M9.
- B2: si la zone walk con 4 zonas se enreda, corre 3 (pres/past/fut perfect) y agrega PERF CONT en la 2a vuelta.
- B3: grupo <=4 -> 2 rotaciones + mas cadena "past simple -> present perfect"; REVIEWER dice 6 estados.
- B4: ticket minimo = 3 frases (1 present perfect, 1 past perfect, 1 future perfect), pero SIEMPRE se recoge.
