### 2B — THE TWO THIRD CONDITIONALS (28')

1. Board — cita del libro (M4 Guia 1 p.29 + Guia 2 p.33), **los dos moldes juntos**:
```
3rd CONDITIONAL — STRAIGHT (B1 Book M4, Guia 1, p.29)
   IF + HAD + participle  ,  WOULD HAVE + participle
   "If I had studied... I would have passed the exam."

3rd CONDITIONAL — MIXED (B1 Book M4, Guia 2, p.33)
   IF + HAD + participle  ,  WOULD + BASE VERB
   "If I had studied... I would work in Rome today."

EL LIBRO LO DICE ASI (p.33):
 "In the 3rd conditional (straight), I did not study in the past and
  consequently did not live in Rome in the PAST.
  In the 3rd conditional (mixed), I did not study in the past and
  consequently did not live in Rome in the PRESENT."
Ambos: la probabilidad es 0% — el pasado no se cambia.
```
2. Señala en 30 seg: *"The 'if' half NEVER changes — HAD + participle, always. Only the second half moves. Dead result, stuck in the past: WOULD HAVE + participle. Missing result, still hurting today: WOULD + base verb. Choose by asking one question: when does the consequence live?"*
3. **ZONE WALK — PAST RESULT vs TODAY (12', DE PIE).** Tablero en dos zonas: `RESULT DIED THEN (straight) | RESULT MISSING NOW (mixed)`. Tu cantas un hecho pasado + una consecuencia ("I didn't learn to program -> I don't work at a tech company today"); las parejas **CAMINAN** a la zona correcta **Y producen la frase completa** ("If I had learned to program, I would work at a tech company today"). El que llega justifica en 1 linea: *"the consequence lives today."* Velocidad creciente; ultimas 4 rondas solo con hechos reales de SU carrera.
4. **Errores tipicos (board, coro 2x):**
   - "if I would have known" -> "if I HAD known" (nunca 'would' tras 'if')
   - "if I had knew" -> "if I had KNOWN" (participio)
   - "If I had studied, I would have work in Rome today" -> mezcla los dos moldes: **o** "would have worked" (entonces) **o** "would work today"
   - "If I had studied, I will be an engineer" -> "I WOULD be an engineer"
5. **Produccion libre (6'):** cada uno toma **un solo hecho real de su pasado** y lo dice **en los dos moldes**: *"If I had taken that job, I would have earned more that year — and I would be a manager today."* Ronda completa, de pie.

### M3 — Deduction (Guia 1 p.23; Ejemplos p.24)
Regla verbatim p.23: *"The verb depends on the certainty the speaker feels: Very certain -> Must ('He must be sick'); Partially certain -> Might / May / Could; Very certain of the opposite -> Can't / Must not."* + *"'He must be playing soccer.' / 'He must have arrived already.'"*
Ejemplos p.24: *"Sam must have arrived last night. His car is here."* · *"The boys can't be playing soccer. I have their ball."*

### M4 Guia 1 — Third Conditional STRAIGHT (p.29; Ejemplos p.30; Ejercicios p.31)
Verbatim p.29: *"The 3rd conditional (straight) refers to something that did (or did not) happen in the past, and therefore, a certain condition did (or did not) exist in the past. Clause with 'if': HAD + PARTICIPLE / Conditional clause: WOULD HAVE + PARTICIPLE. 'If I had studied… I would have passed the exam.' ... The probability of studying is now 0%, because past events cannot be changed."*
Ejemplos p.30: *"If you had woken up on time, we wouldn't have been late."* · *"If the pilots had gone to the airport, they would have arrived on time."* · *"If we had asked the teacher, would he have told us the answer?"*

### M4 Guia 2 — Third Conditional MIXED (p.33; Ejemplos p.34; Ejercicios p.35)
Verbatim p.33: *"The 3rd conditional (mixed) is very similar to the 3rd conditional (straight). But, it refers to something that did (or did not) happen in the past, and therefore, a certain condition does (or does not) exist in the present/future."* — *"If I had studied… I would work in Rome today."* — *"Therefore, I did not study (in the past), and consequently, I do not live in Rome today."*
Contraste verbatim (recuadro p.33): *"In the 3rd conditional (straight), I did not study in the past and consequently did not live in Rome in the PAST. In the 3rd conditional (mixed), I did not study in the past and consequently did not live in Rome in the PRESENT."*
Ejemplos p.34: *"If you had been on time, I would be amazed."* · *"If we hadn't met, my life would be very sad."* · *"If you had lived in Colombia, you would know Spanish."* · *"If the boys had studied medicine, they would be doctors today."* · *"If you had asked for directions, where would you be now?"*

### M4 Guia 3 — The Conditionals Compared (p.37; Ejemplos p.38; Ejercicios p.39)
Verbatim p.37: *"You have now learned all the conditionals! Let's review them all in one table."* · fila 3rd (straight): *"If (past perfect) + (present perfect conditional) — 'If you had helped Ana, she would have been happy.'"* · fila 3rd (mixed): *"If (past perfect) + (conditional) — 'If you had helped Ana, she would be happy.'"* · cierre: *"Remember; the correct conditional depends on the level of probability in the mind of the person talking. The 0, 1st, and 2nd conditionals are used for current or future situations. The 3rd conditionals are used for past situations."*
Ejemplos p.38 (escalera completa): *"0: If I have money, I buy lunch. / 1st: ...I will buy lunch. / 2nd: If I had money, I would buy lunch. / 3rd straight: If I had had money, I would have bought lunch. / 3rd mixed: If I had had money, I would buy lunch."*
