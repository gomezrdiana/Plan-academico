## BLOQUE 2 — HAD + PARTICIPLE EN PAPEL (40') — DE PIE ~90%

**Objective: everyone forms the past perfect and places the flashback in the right event.**

1. Board — cita del libro + regla:
```
PAST PERFECT — HAD + past participle (Libro B1 M9, Guide 2, p.103)
Book (verbatim): "The past perfect tense uses the same participle, but the
  auxiliary verb 'to have' is in the past tense ('had')."
Book: "We use the past perfect together with the simple past to talk about
  events that happened BEFORE another past event."
Book: "The past perfect is a 'narrative tense' because it helps us explain
  the relationship in a story."
Book model: "When we moved to Colombia, I had learned Spanish."
HAD never changes:  I/you/he/she/it/we/they + HAD + participle
Negative: "had not" -> "hadn't"        Question: "Had you finished?" -> "Yes, I had."
Regular participle = -ed (finished, prepared, worked)
Irregular participle (A2 Book M16, p.147): gone, taken, seen, done, been, made
Book examples (p.104, verbatim):
  "I had studied English before I went to London."
  "When I ate dinner, I had already done my homework."
  "Arnulfo arrived early today because he had forgotten to shower."
  "I met my friend Chris after I had studied in Paris."
Contrast: "When I got there, it started" vs "When I got there, it HAD started."
```
2. Señala la logica: *"HAD carries the tense; the verb wears its PARTICIPLE. The book calls it a narrative tense — it is layer five of the story you built yesterday."*
3. **Drill en circulo (10', DE PIE):** cadena — tu lanzas 2 eventos ("arrive / finish the report") -> est. 1 dice cual fue primero, 2 lo une con "before/after", 3 lo une con "because", 4 pregunta + short answer ("Had you finished? Yes, I had."); el 5 lanza el siguiente par. Velocidad creciente.
4. **Errores tipicos al board (coro 2x cada correccion):**
   - "I had finish" -> "I had FINISHED" (participio, no bare)
   - "I had went" -> "I had GONE" (participio irregular, no past simple)
   - "I have studied before I went" -> "I HAD studied before I went" (auxiliar en pasado)
   - HAD en el evento equivocado -> el flashback es lo ANTERIOR, no el principal
5. **Zone walk (12', DE PIE):** tablero `MAIN EVENT (past simple) | EARLIER EVENT (had + participle)`. Dices dos hechos; las parejas CAMINAN colocando cada uno en su zona Y producen la frase enlazada en voz alta con before / after / when / because.
6. Cierre del bloque: cada uno produce de pie 2 frases verdaderas con "had + participle" sobre un logro propio. El uso libre cierra el bloque.
