## BLOQUE 3 — SIMULACION: SHIFT HANDOVER (35') — DE PIE 100%

**Objective: hold the participle and the short answer with proof under pressure, in the moment of the workday where getting it wrong costs money.**

## ANEXO — verbatim del libro, clave de los 8 items y Plan B (no leer en clase)

**A2 Book, Module 16 — "I Have Gone to Paris / The Present Perfect - Irregular" (p.147-150).**
**Objetivo del libro (p.147):** "Expresar frases usando el presente de perfecto con verbos irregulares."
**Guide 1 (p.149), verbatim:** 1. "Aunque muchos verbos (regulares) forman su forma participio agregando '-d' o '-ed', hay un grupo de verbos 'irregulares' que forman el participio en una manera impredecible." 2. "Aunque la unica manera de aprenderlos es por memoria, solo hay alrededor de 30 en uso comun." 3. "Se usa 'have' como auxiliar normal, (recuerde: 'he', 'she' y 'it' usan la forma 'has')."
**Vocabulario del libro (p.148), presente / pasado simple / participio:** be (am-is-are) — was/were — **been** · take (tomar un bus) — took — **taken** · drive (manejar/conducir) — drove — **driven** · wake up (despertarse) — woke up — **woken up** · eat (comer) — ate — **eaten** · go (ir) — went — **gone** · tell (contar/informar) — told — **told** · find (encontrar) — found — **found**.
**Ejemplos del libro (p.150), verbatim:** "I was in Madrid → I have been in Madrid" · "You took a bus to the school → You have taken a bus to the school" · "Sam drove a Suzuki → Sam has driven a Suzuki" · "Daniela woke up at night → Daniela has woken up at night" · "The dog ate meat → The dog has eaten meat" · "We went to Bogota in our car → We have gone to Bogota in our car" · "The children told their parents → The children have told their parents" · "I didn't find my dog → I haven't found my dog" · "You weren't sick → You haven't been sick" · "Fred didn't take a taxi to work → Fred hasn't taken a taxi to work" · "Anita didn't drive a bus → Anita hasn't driven a bus".

**A2 Book, Module 17 (p.163-167).** Guide 1 puntos 1-6, ya trabajados en Gramatica Cl 1 y hablados en Conversacion Cl 1 (y en Conversacion Cl 2): pasado simple = punto especifico ("I studied at 2:00") · presente perfecto = periodo desconocido o no especificado ("I have studied"), accion no terminada ("I have studied [for 2 hours]") o periodo todavia abierto ("I have studied today") · "last year" cerrado vs "this year" abierto · en negativo e interrogativo, *didn't* = no paso y no va a pasar; *haven't* = no ha pasado todavia, pero todavia se puede.
