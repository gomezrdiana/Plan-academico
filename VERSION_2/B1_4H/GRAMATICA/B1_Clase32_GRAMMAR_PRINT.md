## BLOQUE 2 — POSSESSIVE PRONOUNS + PARTICIPLE ADJECTIVES (40') — DE PIE ~90%

**Objective: everyone builds ownership and feeling sentences on paper — mine/whose and -ing/-ed.**

1. Board — cita del libro (M39 + M41, verbatim compacto):
```
OWNERSHIP & FEELINGS (A2 Book M39 + M41)
M39 (Guia p.318-319): "whose" = de quien. Pronombres posesivos:
   mine · yours · his · hers · ours · theirs (se usan solos, sin sustantivo).
   BOOK: "Whose keys are those?" - "They're mine."
         "Whose car is this?" - "It's Frank's."  "Whose hair is longer?" - "Mine is."
M41 (Guia p.327-328): -ING para lo que PRODUCE el efecto,
   -ED para quien lo SIENTE.
   BOOK: "The teacher is boring. The student is bored."
         "He is amazing. She is amazed."  (irregular: "We are lost.")
```
2. **Meta de tarea comunicativa (1 linea):** *"By the end of this block, the board holds 12 real lines — 6 ownership (mine/whose), 6 feelings (3 -ing, 3 -ed)."*
3. **Preparacion en parejas (7', DE PIE junto al board):** cada pareja arma 2 lineas de posesion (whose + pronombre) y 2 de sentimiento (1 -ing, 1 -ed) reales de su trabajo.
4. **Ejecucion (12'):** cada pareja ESCRIBE sus frases en el board, las dice, y el grupo verifica: ¿pronombre solo (mine, no "my")? ¿-ing para la cosa, -ed para la persona? Meta: 12+ limpias.
5. **Errores tipicos (board, coro 2x):**
   - "it's my" (solo) -> "it's mine"
   - "I am boring" (queriendo decir aburrido) -> "I am bored"
   - "the class was tired" -> "the class was tiring"
   - "whose is car?" -> "whose car is it?" (orden)
6. **Producto verificable:** lectura coral del board; feedback primero sobre el logro, luego la forma.

## BLOQUE 3 — DRILL + THE CLAIM & DEBRIEF (35') — DE PIE 100%

**Objective: claim and describe feelings at speed, then hold a realistic project claim + debrief.**

1. **Cadena de conversion (12', circulo, DE PIE):** tu cantas un objeto + dueno ("laptop / her") -> estudiante 1: "Whose laptop is it? It's hers"; 2 agrega un sentimiento con -ing/-ed ("the setup was frustrating; I was frustrated"); 3 lanza el siguiente. Velocidad creciente; ultimo giro: solo cosas reales de SU trabajo.
2. **The claim & debrief (18'):** escenario profesional realista: cierre de proyecto, se reparten materiales y cada quien reporta como se sintio. **1 guest = quien coordina** con el molde (*"Whose reports are these? — They're mine. Whose desk is that? — It's theirs. The week was exhausting and I'm tired, but the result is exciting and the team is proud."*); otro estudiante rota como quien reclama y responde (*"That folder is mine. The process was confusing, but I'm satisfied."*); el resto = **OBSERVERS ACTIVOS** (2 errores, 1 acierto, 1 pregunta). Tu = COACH afuera, nunca guest. 3 rotaciones.
3. **Cierre JUSTICIA (5'):** *"Justice day two — claim one thing that is yours with a pronoun, and name one honest feeling with an -ed word."* — 2 respuestas maximo.
