## BLOQUE 2 — LA HISTORIA POR CAPAS EN PAPEL (40') — DE PIE

**Objective: everyone labels the book's sentences by form and layer, builds a team story on the board without a present leak, and writes a four-layer micro-story.**

1. **Tablero — el molde (4'), DE PIE.** Ninguna forma es nueva:
```
THE STORY MACHINE ON PAPER — one past, no present
(1) SCENE      was / were + verb-ING  + an exact time  (A2 M12 p.100, point 1)
(2) THE TURN   WHEN + past simple                     (A2 M12 p.100, point 2)
(3) STRUGGLE   past simple (regular / irregular) + did / didn't
(4) ORDER      First, / Then, / After that, / Finally,  + last / ago / at / on / in
CHECK EVERY LAYER:  Is the scene -ING?  Is the turn a point?
                    Is there a DIDN'T?  Is any verb in the present?
```
2. **Señala la logica (2'), DE PIE.** *"Each layer has one job and one form. You already own all four. Today they run in a row, with no gaps and no present."*
3. **Los Ejemplos del libro por capa — EN EL PAPEL DEL ESTUDIANTE (10'), DE PIE junto al tablero. Dictado, nada impreso.** Dictas 8 frases del libro (p.101); ellos las escriben y marcan al lado **P (point), L (line) o L+C (line + cut)** y **en que capa** de una historia podria ir (1, 2 o 3). Clave en el ANEXO, no la pongas antes.
```
DICTATION — write it, mark P / L / L+C, and the layer (1, 2 or 3):
 1. I was living in Bucaramanga in 2019.
 2. Eliana was studying for her exams last week.
 3. I was eating pasta when my brother arrived.
 4. Karol didn't want a cookie.
 5. The family was watching a movie when Sebastian left.
 6. Steve woke up at 6:30 today.
 7. The girls weren't driving to school when their dad took a cab.
 8. I was helping Mary, but she felt angry.
```
   Correccion oral en cadena (ultimos 3 min): un estudiante distinto lee cada item y dice su marca. **Items 7 y 8 seguidos:** el 7 es corte con la linea en negativo; el 8 no tiene WHEN pero si linea y punto.
4. **Story-build sprint, DE PIE (10').** Historia colectiva en el tablero sobre **un evento de empresa que casi sale mal**. Antes de cada aporte TU señalas la capa (1, 2, 3 o 4); la pareja de turno pasa, escribe UNA clausula en esa capa y la lee. Meta: **16+ aportes sin un solo verbo en presente**. Si aparece uno, el grupo dice en coro *"Past — all of it"* y la pareja siguiente lo corrige antes de agregar el suyo.
5. **Errores tipicos al tablero (4'), coro 2x cada correccion:**
   - "I was work when he calls" -> "I was WORKING when he CALLED" (forma + fuga al presente)
   - "We worked on the event when the supplier called" (escena en simple) -> "We WERE WORKING on the event when the supplier called"
   - "when the power was going off" (giro en -ing) -> "when the power WENT off"
   - "Finally we finish it" -> "Finally, we FINISHED it"
6. **Layer walk, DE PIE, en parejas (7').** Tablero en 4 zonas: `SCENE | TURN | STRUGGLE | ORDER`. Dictas una linea suelta con un error de forma (*"the clients wait in the lobby" · "when the system was crashing" · "we don't give up" · "finally we sign"*); la pareja **CAMINA** a la capa que le corresponde **Y** la reescribe con la forma correcta **Y** la lee. La pareja vecina verifica con *"Almost — it's..."*.
7. **Cierre del bloque — produccion libre, de pie (3').** Cada estudiante dice su micro-historia verdadera de 4 lineas, una por capa, a un compañero. El drill nunca cierra el bloque: cierra el uso libre.
