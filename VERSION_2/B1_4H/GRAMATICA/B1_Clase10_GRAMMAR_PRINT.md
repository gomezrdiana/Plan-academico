## BLOQUE 2 — SELLAR M16 EN PAPEL Y PREPARAR EL HITO (40') — DE PIE los primeros 30'

**Objective: everyone writes the correct object pronoun, in the affirmative and the negative, after a verb and after a preposition — and leaves with a finished pre-interview note.**

1. **Tablero — la regla del libro (4'), DE PIE.** La clase es 100% ingles; la cita verbatim en espanol la tienes en el ANEXO.
```
OBJECT PRONOUNS — A1 Book M16, Guide 1 (p.199)
1. Different pronouns for the person/thing DOING the action (SUBJECT) and
   the person/thing RECEIVING it (OBJECT).
     SUBJ:  I     you    he    she   it   we   they
     OBJ :  ME    YOU    HIM   HER   IT   US   THEM
     "I love Ana."  ->  "Ana loves ME."
2. "THEM" works for people, animals AND objects.
3. When you replace a noun with an object pronoun, the ORIGINAL ORDER of
   the sentence is ALWAYS kept.
AFTER A PREPOSITION it is also OBJECT:  "I talk with HIM."  "for US."  "to THEM."
NEVER "he helped I"  ·  NEVER "her called me"  ·  NEVER "I like they"
NEVER "I work with he"
```
2. **Señala la logica (2'), DE PIE.** *"Position decides the form. Before the verb it is the subject: SHE called me. After the verb, or after a preposition, it is the object: I called HER, I work with HER. Same person, two shapes."*
3. **Drill en circulo, DE PIE (8').** Cadena cerrada. Tu lanzas una frase con nombre ("My supervisor trained Ana"). Estudiante 1 la reemplaza ("She trained HER"); estudiante 2 la pone en **negativo** ("She didn't train her"); estudiante 3 la convierte en **pregunta** ("Did she train her?"); estudiante 4 da el **short answer** ("Yes, she did.") y lanza la siguiente. Incluye preposiciones ("I work with the two analysts" -> "I work with THEM"). Velocidad creciente; la cadena no se detiene cuando alguien se cae.
4. **Zone walk, DE PIE, en parejas (7').** Tablero en tres zonas: `SUBJECT (before the verb)` · `OBJECT (after the verb)` · `OBJECT (after a preposition)`. Dices una frase con un hueco; cada pareja **CAMINA** a su zona **Y** produce la frase completa, justificando en 1 linea (*"it comes after 'for', so it is object"*). 10 disparadores, velocidad creciente. Al que se equivoca lo corrige la pareja de la zona correcta, no tu.
5. **Ejemplos del libro, p.201 — EN EL PAPEL DEL ESTUDIANTE (9'), DE PIE junto al tablero. Dictado, nada impreso.** Dictas 8 preguntas del libro; ellos escriben **las dos respuestas, afirmativa y negativa**, como las trae el libro. Clave en el ANEXO, no la pongas antes.
```

"Yes, ..." and "No, ...", with the object pronoun.
 1. Do you talk with Omar?                  5. Did you call your mother?

    and Juliet?                             7. Can I help you?
```
