**Objective: everyone produces the book's irregular participles after have / has, in all three forms, without guessing.**

1. **Tablero — cita del libro + tabla (5'), DE PIE.** Las citas verbatim en espanol estan en el ANEXO; al tablero va en ingles:
```
THE PRESENT PERFECT — IRREGULAR (A2 Book M16, p.147-150)
Book (p.149): regular verbs add -d / -ed; IRREGULAR verbs form the
  participle in an unpredictable way. Only memory works — but there
  are only about 30 in common use.
have / has + PARTICIPLE  (he / she / it -> HAS)   "I have eaten pizza."
BOOK TABLE (p.148):  present -> simple past -> PARTICIPLE

```
