## BLOQUE 2 — EL PUNTO Y LA LINEA EN PAPEL (40') — DE PIE

**Objective: everyone writes the past continuous in its four forms, and answers each question with the point or the line it asks for — without being told which.**

1. **Tablero — la tabla (5'), DE PIE.** La clase es 100% ingles; las citas verbatim en espanol las tienes en el ANEXO.
```
POINT OR LINE?  (A2 Book M12, Guide 1 p.100 · A1 M32 p.361 · A1 M33 p.377)
POINT = past simple: "a simple event, finished in the past at a specific
        time"  ->  "I played yesterday."
LINE  = past continuous: "an event in the past that had an extension or
        duration"  ->  "I was playing for three hours."
(+) I / he / she / it WAS + verb-ING      you / we / they WERE + verb-ING
(-) wasn't / weren't + verb-ING           "I wasn't driving the Model T last year."
(?) WAS / WERE + subject + verb-ING?      "Where was Joe working last year?"
    SHORT: "Yes, she was." / "No, they weren't."
-ING SPELLING (A1 M33): ends in E -> drop it: make -> making, drive -> driving
                        some double: swim -> swimming, run -> running
```
2. **Señala la logica (2'), DE PIE.** *"Ask yourself one question: did it finish at that moment, or was it still moving? Point or line. WAS for one person, WERE for you, we, they."*
3. **Drill en circulo, DE PIE (6').** Cadena cerrada. Tu das sujeto + verbo + momento (*"they / load the trucks / at 6:00"*). Estudiante 1 la dice en afirmativo continuo; estudiante 2 en negativo; estudiante 3 la vuelve pregunta y da la short answer; estudiante 4 **deletrea el -ING** (*"L-O-A-D-I-N-G"*) y lanza la siguiente. Mete make, drive, swim, run para la ortografia. Velocidad creciente.
4. **Errores tipicos al tablero (4'), coro 2x cada correccion:**
   - "I was work at 9:00" -> "I was WORKING at 9:00"
   - "They was waiting" -> "They WERE waiting"
   - "Were you working? — Yes, I were." -> "Yes, I WAS."
   - "makeing" / "swiming" -> "MAKING" / "SWIMMING"
   - "I was finishing the report yesterday" (para algo que termino) -> "I FINISHED the report yesterday" (punto)
5. **THE ALIBI FILE — cadena de preguntas, DE PIE (8').** Escribes el expediente (solo las preguntas) y NO dices cual pide punto y cual linea:
```

```
   Circulo: lanzas una Q a un estudiante -> responde en la forma que la pregunta pide -> el de al lado dice en voz alta **"point" o "line"** y por que en 5 palabras -> el tercero lanza la siguiente Q. Velocidad creciente. Di la regla del juego, no la gramatica: *"If the question opens a line, answer with a line. If it closes a point, answer with a point. I will not tell you which is which."*
