- **Vocabulario del modulo (tablero, p.178) (7'):**

  Pronunciacion coral + individual. Ojo con *nurse* (la "r" sin tocar el paladar, truco 7 de M0) y con *an engineer*.
- **Tablero verbatim (M14, Guia 1, p.179, reglas 1-3) (10'):**

```
1. La palabra "with" significa "con". Generalmente se usa
   exactamente como el espanol.
      "Yo deberia estudiar con Gabriela."  ->  "I should study with Gabriela."

2. PERO, en la forma de PREGUNTA, "with" va al FINAL de la frase,
   no al inicio como el espanol.

3. Generalmente, la palabra interrogativa asociada a "with" es
   "who" o "what".

   Who  | Can | Juan | Play | --- | With  ->  Who can Juan play with?
   What | Can | Juan | Play | --- | With  ->  What can Juan play with?

   Espanol:  ?CON quien puede jugar Juan?   (con al INICIO)
   Ingles:   Who can Juan play WITH?        (with al FINAL)
```

  Regla oral unica, con gesto, repetida todo el dia: *"in English, WITH lives at the END of the question."* Empujas la mano al frente cada vez que suene *with*. Coro 3x de las dos filas. El error que vas a cazar hoy: *"With who do you work?"*.
- **Drill de movimiento (15'), TODOS DE PIE en circulo:**

  - Ronda 3 — tiempos mezclados: senalas *present / past / can / will* en el tablero y la cadena produce la misma pregunta en ese tiempo (*"Who do you play with?" · "Who did you play with?" · "Who can you play with?" · "Who will you play with?"*) — *with* no se mueve nunca. ## ANEXO — M14 verbatim + Plan B (no leer en clase)

**Libro A1, Module 14 — "Who Do You Study With? — Use of the Preposition 'With'" (p.177-186). Habilidad objetiva:** "Sin dudar, usar la preposicion 'with' correctamente en preguntas y respuestas."

**Vocabulario (p.178), tal cual — Spanish / English:** Un medico = A doctor · Una enfermera = A nurse · Un piloto = A pilot · Un ingeniero = An engineer · Una secretaria = A secretary · Una empleada = A maid. *(La columna de fonetica del libro va en blanco para que el estudiante la complete.)*

**Guia 1 "La Preposicion 'With'" (p.179), reglas tal cual:**
1. La palabra "with" significa "con". Generalmente, se usa exactamente como el espanol. Ej. *"Yo deberia estudiar con Gabriela." → "I should study with Gabriela."*
2. PERO, en la forma de pregunta, "with" va al final de la frase, no al inicio como el espanol.
3. Generalmente, la palabra interrogativa asociada a "with" es "who" o "what". Descomposicion: *Who → Can → Juan → Play → --- → With → "Who can Juan play with?" (?Con quien puede jugar Juan?)* · *What → Can → Juan → Play → --- → With → "What can Juan play with?" (?Con que puede jugar Juan?)*

**Ejemplos del libro (p.180), los 8 pares tal cual:** *Who do you play with? — I play with Janel. · Who do we eat dinner with? — We eat dinner with Jennifer. · Who do I work with? — You work with Miguel. · What do you play with? — I play with a basketball. · What does Eric play with? — He plays with a football. · Who does Eric play with? — He plays with Richard. · Who can I eat breakfast with? — You can eat breakfast with Harold. · Who should Mike and Mark eat lunch with? — They should eat lunch with Philip.*

**Ejercicios 1 (p.180), respuestas dadas por el libro (la pregunta la construye el estudiante):** *I live with Samuel. · We play with a football. · Frank and Joe eat lunch with Kevin. · I can eat breakfast with Geena. · Betty should arrive with Veronica. · Lucien will live with Lucille.* — y preguntas dadas cuya respuesta construye el estudiante: *Who does the dog play with? · Who can Arnold swim with? · What will the dog play with?* Sirven de disparadores orales del drill.

**Practica Verbal (p.182-186), respuestas modelo del libro para el drill:** *PV1 "Who...with" — Varios Aux (p.182):* "Who will Pepita play with? — She will play with Sarah. · Who should Pepito live with? — He should live with Ginny and Ron. · Who can you work with? — I can work with Karl. · Who can the maid eat lunch with? — She can eat lunch with the doctor. · Who should I study with? — You should study with the engineer. · Who can the nurse work with? — She can work with the doctor." · *PV2 Presente (p.184):* "Who does Pepita play with? — She plays with Sarah. · Who do you work with? — I work with Karl. · Who does the pilot live with? — He lives with Anika." · *PV3 Pasado (p.186):* "Who did Pepita play with? — She played with Sarah. · Who did you work with? — I worked with Karl. · Who did the maid eat lunch with? — She ate lunch with the doctor. · Who did we swim with? — We swam with the secretary." La progresion tipica del libro es la misma pregunta en tres tiempos (*will / do / did*): esa es la ronda 3 del drill.
