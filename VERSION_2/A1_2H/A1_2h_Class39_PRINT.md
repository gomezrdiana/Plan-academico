## BLOQUE 2 (43') — M32: "be" en pasado — WAS y WERE

- **Puente desde Cl 31-32 (4'):** en el tablero, la columna de *be* en presente (*I am · you are · he is · she is · it is · we are · they are*). Al lado, con flecha, la columna del pasado. Una sola linea: *"AM and IS → WAS. ARE → WERE. Only two."*
- **Tablero verbatim (M32, Guia 1, p.361) (12'):**

```
1. "Be" en el pasado se trata como en el presente.
2. El ingles solo tiene UNA forma del pasado: no hay "fue / era / estaba".
3. No hay version abreviada afirmativa. Negativa: "wasn't" (suena "wazunt")
   y "weren't" (suena "wurent").

  ESPANOL                    FULL                  ABREVIADA        PREGUNTA
  Yo (no) fui feliz.         I was (not) happy.    I wasn't happy.     Was I happy?
  Tu (no) fuiste feliz.      You were (not) happy. You weren't happy.  Were you happy?
  El (no) fue feliz.         He was (not) happy.   He wasn't happy.    Was he happy?
  Ella (no) fue feliz.       She was (not) happy.  She wasn't happy.   Was she happy?
  [Eso] (no) fue feliz.      It was (not) happy.   It wasn't happy.    Was it happy?
  Nosotros (no) fuimos...    We were (not) happy.  We weren't happy.   Were we happy?
  Ell@s (no) fueron...       They were (not) happy.They weren't happy. Were they happy?

4. Respuesta corta como siempre: Yes, I was. / No, they weren't.
```

  Vocabulario (p.360) en una columna al lado: *yesterday · last night · last week · last weekend · last month · last year · last class.* Coro 3x por fila, siempre en trio presente → pasado → pregunta (*"He is sick. He was sick. Was he sick?"*). El error que vas a cazar: *"I were" / "he were" / "you was"*, *"Did you be...?"* (reflejo de Cl 17-20) y *"yesterday night"* (el libro: LAST NIGHT).
- **Historia interactiva (17'):** TU narras "Diego no fue a la oficina ayer" (el titulo del modulo), con *was / were* + palabras de tiempo. Narras 2-3 frases (*"Diego was sick yesterday. He wasn't in the office. Was he in the hospital? No, he wasn't! He was in his house... with his dog. And the boss? The boss was really angry. The engineers were busy, and the secretary was worried."*) → PAUSA → el grupo repite el fragmento en coro → un estudiante AGREGA donde o como estaba otro personaje (*"The pilot was in London last week."*) y el de al lado le pregunta (*"Was he happy?" — "No, he wasn't. He was tired."*); TU lo tejes. Cada 2-3 pausas lanzas *"Was Diego sick yesterday?"* y el coro responde *"Yes, he was!"*. NUNCA huecos para llenar. Cierre: 2-3 estudiantes recuentan la historia de ayer en la oficina.
- **Meta en 1 linea (di al inicio) (10'):** *"By the end of this block, each of you will say where you were and how you were at 3 real moments — yesterday, last weekend, last year — and ask a partner 2 questions."* Preparacion en parejas (4', oral, de pie) → ejecucion ante el grupo (*"Last weekend I was in Giron.
