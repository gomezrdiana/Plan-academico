```
REGLA CLAVE: el "-ed" SOLO vive en la afirmativa.
En la NEGATIVA y en la PREGUNTA vuelve el auxiliar "did" + verbo BASE.

NEGATIVA
Sujeto | Ayudante | Not | Verbo | Complemento
Juan   | did      | not | play  | soccer  ->  Juan did not play soccer.
                                         ->  Juan didn't play soccer.  ("dedunt")

PREGUNTA
Ayudante | Sujeto | Verbo | Complemento
Did      | Juan   | play  | soccer?  ->  Did Juan play soccer?

RESPUESTA CORTA          PALABRA DE PREGUNTA NUEVA
Yes, he did.             who = quien  ("ju")
No, he didn't.           Who did Roddy love? - He loved Alina.
```

## ANEXO — M10 verbatim (parte 2) + Plan B (no leer en clase)

**Libro A1, Module 10 — "What Time Did You Arrive? — The Simple Past for Regular Verbs" (p.123-140). Habilidad objetiva:** "Sin dudar, responder a preguntas usando la respuesta corta."

**Vocabulario (p.124), tal cual (visto en Cl 17, se recicla hoy):** Gustar = Like · Amar = Love · Llegar = Arrive · Visitar = Visit · Ayudar = Help.

**Guia 1 (p.125), reglas tal cual:**
2. El auxiliar "did" tiene la misma propiedad especial que "do/does": en el caso positivo, (NUNCA EN EL CASO NEGATIVO O DE LA PREGUNTA) generalmente lo omitimos y correr la "-d" o la "-ed" al verbo. Este sistema NO funciona para todos los verbos.
3. La pronunciacion de la "-d" o "-ed" depende en el ultimo sonido del verbo: *-t/-d* → "-ed" (*wanted* = "wanTED", *waded* = "wayDED") · *consonante + y* → "-id" (*studied* = "staDID", *partied* = "parTID") · *vocal o consonante suave* → "-d" (*played* = "play'D", *lived* = "lev'D") · *consonante dura* → "-t" (*jumped* = "yamp'T", *worked* = "work'T").
4. En su forma abreviada "did + not" = "didn't" (pronunciada "dedunt").
5. La palabra interrogativa "who" (pronunciada "ju") significa "quien".

**Ejemplos del libro (p.126):** *What did Juan play? — He played soccer. · What time did the dog arrive? — It arrived at 1:00. · Where did Ana shower? — She showered in the house. · Where did Mafe work? — She worked in the office. · Who did Roddy love? — He loved Alina. · Who did Jefferson help? — He helped Jonathan. · Who did you visit? — I visited Felicity. · Did Freddy arrive? — No, he didn't arrive. · Did they visit? — Yes, they did. / No, they didn't. · Did we help the dog? — Yes, we did. / No, we didn't. · Did John work with Steve? — Yes, he did. / No, he didn't.*

**Practica Verbal (encabezados, p.131-138):** *PV2 "Did" Negativo (crear la frase CON "did", 45 seg) · PV3 Interrogativo · PV4 Mezclados · PV5 crear la pregunta y generar la RESPUESTA CORTA usando el pronombre (60 seg).* Respuestas modelo del libro para el drill: *"He didn't play badminton." · "We didn't like basketball." · "Did you work in the office?" · "Did she love Pepita?" · "Did Pepita play golf? — No, she didn't." · "Did Pepito live in Madrid? — Yes, he did." · "Did Valeria and Manuel help Pepita? — Yes, they did."*
