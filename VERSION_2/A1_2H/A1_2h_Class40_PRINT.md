## BLOQUE 2 (43') — M33: el continuo — lo que pasa AHORA

- **Puente desde Cl 13-16 y Cl 31 (4'):** escribes *"I play soccer."* (presente simple, Cl 13-16: lo que hago normalmente) y debajo *"I'm playing golf today."* Una sola linea, con el ejemplo del libro (regla 1): *"Yo juego futbol, pero estoy jugando golf hoy."* Nada mas — la comparacion completa es de Cl 41.
- **Tablero verbatim (M33, Guia 1, p.377) (12'):**

```
1. Presente simple = acciones habituales. Continuo = lo que estamos
   haciendo en este momento.
   "I play soccer, but I am playing golf today."
2. Se forma con "be" (estar) + verbo + "-ing" (= -ando / -iendo).
   Yo  estoy  jug + ando    ->  I  am  play + ing  ->  I am playing.

  INTERROGATIVO                AFIRMATIVO / NEGATIVO
  Am I playing soccer?         I am (not) playing soccer.
  Are you playing soccer?      You are (not) playing soccer.
  Is he playing soccer?        He is (not) playing soccer.
  Is she playing soccer?       She is (not) playing soccer.
  Is it playing soccer?        It is (not) playing soccer.
  Are we playing soccer?       We are (not) playing soccer.
  Are they playing soccer?     They are (not) playing soccer.

3. Verbo termina en "e": se quita la e.      like -> liking
4. Algunos verbos doblan la consonante.       run  -> running
5. Contracciones y respuesta corta: las de "be".
   "Are you playing today?" -> "Yes, I am." / "No, I'm not."
```

  Vocabulario (p.376) en una columna al lado: *take a bus · take a taxi · drive · walk · watch · clean.* Coro 3x por fila en trio afirmativa → negativa → pregunta, con la contraccion (*"He's playing. He isn't playing. Is he playing?"*). El error que vas a cazar: *"I playing"* (sin *be*), *"I am play"* (sin *-ing*), *"Do you playing?"* y *"driveing"*.

## ANEXO — M33 verbatim + Plan B (no leer en clase)

**Libro A1, Module 33 — "What Are You Doing? — The Continuous" (p.375-394). Habilidades objetivas (p.375):** "Sin dudar, aprender el vocabulario del modulo." · "Sin dudar, formar frases y preguntas usando el presente continuo." · "Sin dudar, distinguir entre el presente simple y el presente continuo."

**Guia 1 "El Continuo" (p.377), tal cual:** 1. Mientras el presente simple se usa para expresar acciones habituales, el continuo se usa para acciones que nos encontramos realizando en el presente momento. Ej. "Yo juego futbol, pero estoy jugando golf hoy." → "I play soccer, but I am playing golf today." 2. Se forma con "be" ("estar") de auxiliar y agregando "-ing" al verbo (el equivalente de "-ando" o "-iendo"). 3. Cuando el verbo termina en "e", la quitamos para agregar el "-ing". E.j. Like → Liking. 4. Por razones que explicamos mas tarde, en algunos casos agregamos una segunda consonante antes de agregar el "-ing". Ej. Run → Running. 5. Para las abreviaciones y respuesta corta, las mismas reglas aplican de "be" que ya has aprendido. Ej. "Are you playing today?" "Yes, I am." / "No, I'm not."

**Ejemplos (p.378), seleccion tal cual:** *Are you taking a bus today? — No, I'm taking a taxi. · Is Miguel taking a taxi today? — No, he is taking a bus. · Where is Maria walking? — She is walking in the park. · What are you cleaning? — I am cleaning my house. · Where is Steven driving? — He is driving to the office. · Who is working in the office? — Johanna is working in the office. · What time are the girls playing? — They're playing at 8:30.*

**Practica Verbal (p.381-394):** PV1 afirmativo abreviado, 45 seg (*He's playing badminton. · We're playing basketball.*) · PV2 negativo, 45 seg (*He's not playing badminton. / He isn't playing badminton.*) · PV3 preguntas, 45 seg (*Is he playing badminton? · Am I playing hockey?*) · PV4 mezclados, 45 seg · PV5 verbos adicionales, 50 seg (*Is Pepita taking a bus? · The maid is watching a movie. · Are we cleaning our house?*) · PV6 respuesta corta, 1 min 15 seg (*Is Dayra playing tennis? — No, she's not / No, she isn't. · Are Valeria and Manuel driving to the house? — Yes, they are.*) · PV7 *who...with / where / what time / what*, 1 min 30 seg (*Who is the maid calling? — She's calling the secretary. · What time is the nurse showering? — She's showering at three fifteen.*)
