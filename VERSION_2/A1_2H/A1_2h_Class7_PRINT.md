## BLOQUE 2 (43') — M4 parte 2: "where" + respuesta corta + "in"

- **Tablero verbatim (M4, Guia 1, p.43, reglas 4-6) (10'):**

```
PREGUNTA CON PALABRA INTERROGATIVA
Palabra Interr. -> Ayudante | Sujeto | Verbo | Complemento
Where           -> Should   | Juan   | live  | ---     ->  Where should Juan live?

"where" (pronunciada "wear") = "donde".

RESPUESTA CORTA: igual que con "can", es simplemente "should" y "shouldn't".
   Should Freddy live in Colombia?  ->  Yes, he should. / No, he shouldn't.

"in" = "en".   Ej. "...in Bucaramanga",  "...in Colombia."
```

  Contrasta con lo de Cl 4: *what* pregunta por la COSA, *where* pregunta por el LUGAR. Coro 3x con *"What should he play?"* / *"Where should he live?"*.
- **Ejemplos al tablero (p.44-45), coro + individuales:** *Where should Martin live? — He should live in London. · Where should Sandra live? — She should live in Paris. · Where should Donald and Douglas live? — They should live in New York. · Where should Julius live? — He should live in Rome. · Where should James live? — He should live in Madrid. · Where should Walter live? — He should live in Orlando.*
- **Historia interactiva (18'):** TU narras una historia absurda: la familia del edificio tiene que mudarse y cada quien opina donde deberia vivir cada quien. Narras 2-3 frases (*"The grandmother should live in Paris. She shouldn't live in New York..."*) → PAUSA → el grupo repite el fragmento en coro → en cada pausa lanzas la pregunta (*"Where should the dog live?"*) y un estudiante AGREGA la respuesta con *should + live in + ciudad*, que TU tejes al siguiente tramo. Mete tambien respuestas cortas (*"Should he live in Rome?" — "No, he shouldn't."*). NUNCA huecos para llenar. Cierre: 2-3 estudiantes recuentan donde termino viviendo cada personaje.
- **Meta en 1 linea (di al inicio):** *"By the end of this block, each of you will ask 1 question with WHERE, answer it with IN, and give 2 short answers with should."* Preparacion en parejas (6', oral) → ejecucion en cadena → feedback: logro primero, forma despues.

## ANEXO — M4 verbatim (parte 2) + Plan B (no leer en clase)

**Libro A1, Module 4 — "Where Should We Live? — Giving Recommendations" (p.41-60). Habilidades objetivas:** "Sin dudar, demuestras que has aprendido el vocabulario del modulo. Sin dudar, respondes de forma positiva y negativa a preguntas sobre recomendaciones."

**Guia 1 (p.43), reglas 4-6 tal cual:**
4. La palabra de pregunta "where" (pronunciada "wear") significa "donde". Palabra Interr. → Ayudante + Sujeto + Verbo + Complemento: *"Where should Juan play?"* = "Donde deberia jugar Juan?"
5. Igual a "can", la respuesta corta es simplemente "should" y "shouldn't".
6. La palabra "in" significa "en". Por ejemplo, "...en Bucaramanga", "...en Colombia".

**Ejemplos del libro (p.44-45), tal cual:** *Where should Martin live? — He should live in London. · Where should Sandra live? — She should live in Paris. · Where should Donald and Douglas live? — They should live in New York. · Where should Julius live? — He should live in Rome. · Where should James live? — He should live in Madrid. · Where should Walter live? — He should live in Orlando. · Where should Simon live? — He should live in Venice. · Should Freddy live in Colombia? — Yes, he should. / No, he shouldn't. · Should Sarah live in Paris? — Yes, she should. / No, she shouldn't. · Should we live in San Gil? — Yes, we should. / No, we shouldn't. · Should the dog live in Bucaramanga? — Yes, it should. / No, it shouldn't. · Where should Pedro live? — He should live in Canaveral. · Where can Pedro live? — He can live in Canaveral. · Where should Maria play? — She should play in Rome. · Where can Maria play? — She can play in London. · Where should Stephanie and Ana live? — They should live in Lebrija. · Where can Stephanie and Ana live? — They can live in Giron.*
