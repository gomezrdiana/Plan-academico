## BLOQUE 2 (43') — M35: "going to" — el plan

- **Puente desde Cl 40-41 (3'):** en el tablero: *"I am playing."* Debajo, agregas *going to*: *"I am GOING TO play."* Una sola linea, es la regla 2 del libro: *"It's 'go' with -ing — plus a verb."*
- **Vocabulario del modulo (tablero, p.404) (6'):**

```
Buy     comprar      (past: bought)      Come     venir       (past: came)
Feel    sentirse     (past: felt)        Meet     conocerse / reunirse (past: met)
Travel  viajar                           Finish   terminar
```

  Pronunciacion coral + individual. Ojo con la consonante final fuerte (truco 2 de Cl 1): *feel · travel · meet*.
- **Tablero verbatim (M35, Guia 1, p.405) (10'):**

```
1. Para los eventos fijos en el futuro, el espanol usa la construccion
   "ir a" e ingles "to be going to".
2. De forma, la construccion es simplemente "go" en presente continuo
   con un verbo adicional.

  AFIRMATIVO / NEGATIVO               INTERROGATIVO
  I am (not) going to play.           Am I going to play?
  You are (not) going to play.        Are you going to play?
  He is (not) going to play.          Is he going to play?
  She is (not) going to play.         Is she going to play?
  It is (not) going to play.          Is it going to play?
  We are (not) going to play.         Are we going to play?
  They are (not) going to play.       Are they going to play?

3. Las palabras "going to" a menudo salen rapidamente como "gonna".
   Esto es comun en conversacion, pero no en trabajo escrito.
   "I'm going to play."  ->  "I'm gonna play."
```

  Coro 3x de la tabla en par afirmativa → pregunta, con la mano marcando el salto de *am / is / are* (igual que Cl 32). Luego 2x en *gonna*, SOLO oral: *"Speaking: gonna. Writing: going to."* Los errores que vas a cazar hoy: *"I going to play"* (sin *be*), *"I'm going to playing"*, *"Are you going to plays?"* y *gonna* escrito.
- **Historia interactiva (14'):** TU narras "el viaje de negocios de manana: el plan que todos van a cumplir", con personajes cotidianos y verbos del modulo. Narras 2-3 frases (*"Tomorrow the boss is going to travel to Canada. He's going to meet a client at 9:15. But the secretary is going to buy the tickets... for Orlando! The engineer isn't going to come. And the dog? The dog is going to finish the report!"*) → PAUSA → el grupo repite el fragmento en coro → un estudiante AGREGA el plan de otro personaje (*"The nurse is going to buy some tomatoes."*) y TU lo tejes. Cada 2-3 pausas lanzas una pregunta (*"Is the engineer going to come?"*) y el coro responde con frase completa. NUNCA huecos para llenar. Cierre: 2-3 estudiantes recuentan el plan completo.
- **Meta en 1 linea (di al inicio) (10'):** *"By the end of this block, each of you will say 3 real plans with going to — 1 negative — and ask 1 question."* Preparacion en parejas (4', oral, de pie) → ejecucion ante el grupo (*"Tomorrow I'm going to work at seven. I'm not going to take a taxi. Are you going to come to class?"*) → feedback: logro primero, forma despues.
