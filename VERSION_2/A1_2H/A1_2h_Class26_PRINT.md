- **Tablero verbatim (M17, Guia 1, p.210-211, reglas 1-7) (13'):**

```
1. En el presente simple afirmativo para "he", "she" e "it", NO decimos
   "haves" sino "HAS".  (Pasado: "had".)

      AFIRMATIVO          NEGATIVO                 INTERROGATIVO
   I  have a car.      I don't have a car.      Do I have a car?
   He HAS a car.       He doesn't have a car.   Does he have a car?

2. Para cantidades SINGULARES, usamos "a/an" antes del objeto.
      I have an apple. / I don't have an apple. / Do I have an apple?

3. Para elementos PLURALES O INCONTABLES: "SOME" en el afirmativo,
   "ANY" en el negativo y el interrogativo. (Parecido a "alguna/ninguna".)
      I have SOME apples. / I don't have ANY apples. / Do I have ANY apples?
      I have SOME water.  / I don't have ANY water.  / Do I have ANY water?

4. "For"  -> "para".    I have something for you.
5. "Or"   -> "o".       Do you have a hat or an apple?
6. "Only" -> "solo". Va ANTES del verbo principal.  I only have a hat.
7. "Too"  -> "tambien". Va AL FINAL de la frase.    I have a car, too.
```

  Regla oral unica, repetida todo el dia: *"SOME says yes — ANY says no or asks."* Coro 3x de la fila triple (afirmativo / negativo / interrogativo). Dos errores que vas a cazar hoy: *"He haves"* y *"I don't have some water"*.
- **Historia interactiva (15'):** TU narras "la tienda del edificio que nunca tiene nada", todo con *have / has / some / any*. Narras 2-3 frases (*"The shop has some coffee. It doesn't have any water. The grandmother has some money for the dog — but the dog only has a sandwich!"*) → PAUSA → el grupo repite el fragmento en coro → un estudiante AGREGA que tiene o que no tiene alguien de la historia, y TU lo tejes eligiendo *some* o *any* segun la forma y marcandolo con la mano (pulgar arriba = some, pulgar abajo = any). Cada 2-3 pausas lanzas *"Does the shop have any ice cream?"* y el coro responde con la respuesta corta. NUNCA huecos para llenar. Cierre: 2-3 estudiantes recuentan que tiene y que no tiene la tienda.
- **Meta en 1 linea (di al inicio) (7'):** *"By the end of this block, each of you will say 2 things you HAVE with some, 2 things you DON'T HAVE with any, and will ask 1 question with any."* Preparacion en parejas (4', oral) → ejecucion ante el grupo → feedback: logro primero, forma despues.

## BLOQUE 3 (37') — Drill de pie + Simulacion

- **Drill de movimiento (15'), TODOS DE PIE en circulo:**
  - Ronda 1 — las tres formas: dices sujeto + objeto (*"he / coffee" · "they / questions" · "she / a sandwich"*) y la cadena produce la afirmativa, la negativa y la pregunta seguidas, eligiendo *a/an*, *some* o *any* segun toque (*"He has some coffee." → "He doesn't have any coffee." → "Does he have any coffee?"*). Quien lo hace sin decir *"haves"* cambia de lugar en el circulo. Velocidad creciente.
  - Ronda 2 — contable vs incontable: escribes dos columnas en el tablero (CONTABLE: *a sandwich · a question · vegetables · hamburgers* — INCONTABLE: *money · pasta · water · coffee · ice cream · fruit*). Senalas una palabra al azar y la cadena produce la afirmativa correcta; el siguiente la vuelve negativa. Si la palabra es incontable y alguien dice *"a water"*, se corrige en el acto y sigue la cadena.
  - Ronda 3 — *or · only · too*: cada estudiante pregunta al siguiente con *or* (*"Do you have any soda or any coffee?"*) y este responde con *only* (*"I only have some coffee."*); un tercero se suma con *too* al final (*"I have some coffee, too."*). Cierre: 2 min de produccion libre, cada uno 2 frases reales de lo que tiene y no tiene.
- **Simulacion (17'): THE CORNER SHOP — LA LISTA DEL MERCADO.** Escenario cotidiano montado solo con tablero y sala: alguien entra a la tienda de la esquina con una lista y el tendero responde que hay y que no hay. En el tablero escribes ANTES las 5 preguntas guia: *Do you have any water? · Do you have any fruit or any vegetables? · Does the shop have any ice cream? · Do you have a sandwich for me? · How much money do you have?*
  - Al frente: **1 estudiante es EL CLIENTE (guest)** + 1 estudiante es EL TENDERO. El cliente pregunta SIEMPRE con *any* (*"Do you have any pasta?"*); el tendero responde con la respuesta corta + la frase completa con *some* o *any* segun toque (*"Yes, I do. We have some pasta." / "No, I don't. We don't have any pasta."*) y usa al menos una vez *only* y una vez *or*; el cliente cierra diciendo en tercera persona que se lleva y que no (*"The shop has some pasta. It doesn't have any ice cream. I only have some money for the fruit."*).
  - El RESTO son OBSERVERS ACTIVOS: anotar 2 errores (sobre todo *some/any* cruzados y *"he haves"*), 1 acierto, 1 pregunta.
  - TU eres COACH desde afuera: NUNCA juegas cliente ni tendero. Congelados 15+ segundos → UN prompt (*"some or any?"*) y te retiras.
  - Minimos por ronda: 4 preguntas con *any* + 2 respuestas afirmativas con *some* + 2 negativas con *any* + 1 frase con *only* + 1 con *or* + el resumen final en tercera persona.
  - Rotacion cada 3 min hasta que todos pasen. Debrief (4'): observers reportan (anonimo); la correccion nuclear al tablero — *"I don't have some water"* → *"I don't have ANY water"*, coro 2x.
- **MY WORLD — avance de proyecto hoy (5'): pieza 23 "WHAT I HAVE AND WHAT I DON'T HAVE".** Cada estudiante dice al grupo 4 frases reales: 2 con *some* (afirmativas) + 2 con *any* (negativas), minimo 1 incontable, y le lanza 1 pregunta *"Do you have any ...?"* a un companero. Esa pieza es el audio de hoy.

Habilidades objetivas:** "Sin dudar, conjugar y usar el verbo 'to be' correctamente en todos los tiempos." · "Sin dudar, diferenciar entre los adjetivos indefinidos 'any' y 'some' y formar frases afirmativos, negativos e interrogativos usandolos mismos."

**Vocabulario (p.210), tal cual — Spanish / English:** Dinero = Money · Una pregunta = A question · Pasta = Pasta · Un sandwich = A sandwich · Vegetales / Verduras = Vegetables · Fruta = Fruit · Helado = Ice cream · Agua = Water · Gaseosa = Soda · Cafe = Coffee. *(La columna de fonetica del libro va en blanco para que el estudiante la complete.)*

**Guia 1 "'Have' y Adjetivos Indefinidos" (p.210-211), reglas tal cual:**
1. En el presente simple afirmativo para "he", "she" e "it", no decimos "haves" sino "has". (Pasado: "had".) Tabla: *I have a car. / I don't have a car. / Do I have a car?* · *He has a car. / He doesn't have a car. / Does he have a car?*
2. Para cantidades singulares, usamos "a/an" antes del objeto: *I have an apple. / I don't have an apple. / Do I have an apple?*
3. Para elementos PLURALES O INCONTABLES, se usa "some" en el caso afirmativo y "any" en el negativo e interrogativo. (Parecido a "alguna/ninguna".) *I have some apples. / I don't have any apples. / Do I have any apples?* · *I have some water. / I don't have any water. / Do I have any water?*
4. "For" → "para". Ej. *"I have something for you."* = "Tengo algo para ti."
5. "Or" → "o". Ej. *"Do you have a hat or an apple?"* = "?Tienes un gorro o una manzana?"
6. "Only" → "solo". Se coloca antes del verbo principal o despues de "to be". Ej. *"I only have a hat."* = "Solo tengo un gorro."
7. "Too" → "tambien". Se coloca al fin de la frase. Ej. *"I have a car, too."* = "Yo tengo un carro, tambien."

**Ejemplos del libro (p.211-212), seleccion tal cual:** *Do you have any pizza? — Yes, I have some pizza. / No, I don't have any pizza. · Does Fritz have a hamburger? — Yes, he has a hamburger. / No, he doesn't have a hamburger. · Does Harold have any pasta? — Yes, he has some pasta. / No, he doesn't have any pasta. · Do you have a question? — Yes, I have a question. · Does Jenni have any questions? — Yes, she has some questions. · Do Jenni and Olaf have any vegetables? — Yes, they have some vegetables. · Do we have any fruit? — Yes, we have some fruit. · Do you have any soda or coffee? — I only have some soda. · Do they have a sandwich or a hotdog? — They only have a sandwich. / They have a sandwich and a hotdog. · Do your parents have any money for you? — Yes, they have some money for me. · Does the class have any questions for the teacher? — Yes, they have some questions for him. · Does the restaurant have any ice cream? — No, it doesn't have any ice cream. · Do you have a hot dog or a hamburger? — I only have a hamburger. · Do you have any money in your house? — Yes, I have some money in my house. · Does the team have any broccoli? — Yes, they have some broccoli. · I have a car. — My sister has a car, too. · We have some money. — The class has some money, too.*
