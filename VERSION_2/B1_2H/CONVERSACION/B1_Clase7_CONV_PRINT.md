## BLOQUE 2 — HOW OFTEN, EN VOZ ALTA (45')

**Objective: by the end, everyone describes their week with the five adverbs in the correct position, and answers "How often...?" without hesitating.**

1. **Avance de proyecto (2'): MY STORY, MY GOALS — piece 6 "The habit I always keep and the one I never break".** Di: *"Piece 6: two sentences — one habit you ALWAYS keep and one thing you NEVER do, because both are building your English. This goes into tonight's video."*
2. **Tablero (3') — solo uso ORAL; la regla la formaliza Gramatica la proxima noche:**
```
HOW OFTEN?   always -> usually -> often -> sometimes -> never
POSITION: right BEFORE the main verb.
   "I ALWAYS confirm by email."      "She USUALLY arrives at nine."
   "He OFTEN calls before lunch."    "We SOMETIMES work on the last day."
   "They NEVER close on time."
With BE the adverb goes AFTER:   "She is USUALLY on time."
QUESTION: "HOW OFTEN do you check email?" -> "I check it every morning."
Third person keeps its -s:  "she usually ARRIVES", never "she usually arrive".
```
3. **Modelas 60 seg (2'):** tu semana real con los cinco adverbios, mas 2 frases con BE. Señalas: *"The adverb sits right before the main verb — 'I ALWAYS confirm', not 'I confirm always'. Only with BE does it move after — 'she is usually calm'."* **No explicas la regla del libro.**
4. **Drill de movimiento, de pie (9') — dos zonas.** Rotula el tablero `BEFORE THE MAIN VERB` y `AFTER BE`. Lanzas sujeto mas verbo mas adverbio ("he / call / often"; "she / be / usually late"); los estudiantes **CAMINAN** a su zona Y producen la frase completa en voz alta; el que llega justifica en 1 linea. Velocidad creciente; ultimas rondas con verbos de SU trabajo. El drill NO cierra el bloque.
5. **Preparacion en parejas (7', notas minimas en su propio papel).** Cada uno arma **8 frases sobre su semana** usando los cinco adverbios (al menos 2 con BE y 3 de tercera persona con -s) mas **4 preguntas "How often do you...?"** y **piece 6**. TEMPLANZA: el drill completo, sin atajos.
6. **Entrevista en parejas, 2 rondas (14').** Ronda 1: A pregunta *"How often do you...?"* seis veces; B responde en frase completa con adverbio en posicion. Cambian. Ronda 2 con pareja nueva, mas rapida y sin notas. Numero impar = un trio. TODOS preguntan y responden dos veces.
