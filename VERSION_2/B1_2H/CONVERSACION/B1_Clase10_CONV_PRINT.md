## BLOQUE 2 — REEMPLAZA EL NOMBRE Y ARMA TU RESPUESTA (40')

**Objective: by the end, every one of you replaces names with the 7 object pronouns out loud, and has a 90-second answer to "Tell me about yourself" ready to defend.**

1. **Avance de proyecto hoy: MY STORY, MY GOALS — pieza "TELL ME ABOUT YOURSELF" (1').** Di: *"One piece every class. Tonight's piece is the one you will use for the rest of your life: ninety seconds about who you are, what you have done, and where you are going. It is also the first question of your interview in twenty minutes, and the opening of your midterm presentation."*
2. **Tablero, solo uso ORAL (4') — la tabla formal la sella Gramatica Cl 10:**
```
SUBJECT -> OBJECT  (after the VERB, or after a PREPOSITION)
   I -> ME     you -> YOU    he -> HIM    she -> HER
   it -> IT    we -> US      they -> THEM
   "I know Camilo."          -> "I know HIM."
   "Can you help my mother?" -> "Yes, I can help HER."
   "Do you like pizza?"      -> "Yes, I like IT."
   "...hamburgers?"          -> "Yes, I like THEM."
   "Do you talk with Omar?"  -> "Yes, I talk with HIM."   (preposition)
KEEP THE ORDER of the sentence -- only the noun becomes a pronoun.
NEVER "he helped I"  ·  NEVER "her called me"  ·  NEVER "I like they"
```
3. **Las 6 frases del dia al tablero (4') — texto de lectura de Heiiu sobre mentores, dictado, cero material impreso.** Escribelas, el grupo las lee en coro, y cada estudiante subraya en su propio papel los pronombres objeto:
```
1. "When someone admires you, they tell YOU what you want to hear."
2. "When a mentor respects you, they tell YOU what you need to hear."
3. "A teacher who tells US our work is mediocre has taught US more."
4. "They want US to succeed, but they also want US to leave THEM."
5. "Their mission ends the day we no longer need THEM."
6. "Who is incentivized to tell YOU the uncomfortable truth?"
```
   Cada estudiante resume la idea en 2 frases propias con 2 pronombres objeto (*"A mentor tells US the truth; a friend protects US"*). NO se debate el texto: es material de produccion, no de opinion.
4. **Modelas 60 seg (2').** Cuentas quien te enseño lo que sabes, reemplazando 4 nombres reales por pronombres. Señalas la posicion con el dedo; NO explicas la regla.
5. **Drill de movimiento, DE PIE (8').** Divide el tablero en dos zonas rotuladas `SUBJECT (before the verb)` y `OBJECT (after verb / after preposition)`. Dices una frase con un nombre ("My supervisor trained Ana", "Ana called my supervisor", "I work with the two analysts"); los estudiantes **CAMINAN** a la zona del hueco que hay que reemplazar **Y** producen la frase completa con el pronombre; el que llega justifica en 1 linea (*"it comes after 'with', so it is object"*). Velocidad creciente. El drill NO cierra el bloque.
6. **Preparacion de la respuesta, en parejas (10', notas minimas en su propio papel).** Cada uno arma su **"Tell me about yourself"** de 90 segundos con esta estructura en el tablero:
```

     "My manager asked ME to..."  "I trained THEM."

 RULES: at least 4 object pronouns. No reading. 90 seconds, not 4 minutes.
```
7. **Ensayo cruzado y producto (11').** Ronda 1: A dice su respuesta a B en 90 seg; B cronometra con los dedos y le devuelve UNA cosa (*"you never said what you have done"*). Cambian. Cambio de pareja, ronda 2 mas corta y mas firme. Luego 3-4 estudiantes la dicen al grupo. Feedback primero sobre el LOGRO (*"did I want to hire you?"*), despues sobre la forma. Errores en 2+ parejas al tablero ANONIMOS, coro 2x; nombres y citas a tu cuaderno.

## BLOQUE 3 — HITO: SIMULATED JOB INTERVIEW (45')

**Objective: hold a real three-minute job interview in English, under pressure, using the pronouns and everything this block trained — and leave it recorded.**

