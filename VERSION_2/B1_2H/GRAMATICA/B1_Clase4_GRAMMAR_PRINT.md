## BLOQUE 2 — SELLAR WILL Y LAS TRES PREPOSICIONES EN PAPEL (45') — DE PIE ~90%

```
"WILL" — THE SIMPLE FUTURE (A1 Book M6, Guia 1, p.73)
THE BOOK SAYS: it is treated EXACTLY like "can" and "should".
 (+) SUBJECT + will + VERB + complement -> "Juan will play soccer."
 (-) will + not = won't (say "wont")     -> "Juan won't play soccer."
 (?) WILL + subject + VERB + complement? -> "Will Juan play soccer?"
```
