## BLOQUE 2 — SELLAR M13 EN PAPEL (45') — DE PIE ~90%

**Objective: everyone builds the expression correctly, places it at the end, and holds it with any auxiliary — fast, and written.**

```
EXPRESSIONS OF FREQUENCY — A1 Book M13, Guide 1 (p.167)
1. Frequency expressions also say HOW OFTEN an event happens.
2. SHAPE:  FREQUENCY  +  A(N)  +  PERIOD
      once (1x) / twice (2x) / three times (3x) / four times (4x)
      + A +  day / week / month / year
3. "EVERY" = "cada".   every + SINGULAR period:  every day / every week
      -> "every" takes NO "a".      NEVER "every days".
4. The expression goes at the END of the sentence, exactly as in Spanish.
```
2. **Tablero 2 — la maquina con auxiliares (5'). Esta es la habilidad objetiva del modulo: la expresion con VARIOS auxiliares.**
```
THE COUNT SURVIVES ANY AUXILIARY -- and stays at the END
PRESENT    I work in the office        twice a month.
DO/DOES    How often DOES he play tennis?   -> He plays tennis once a year.
CAN        We CAN visit the site       four times a day.
SHOULD     We SHOULD swim              three times a week.
WILL       They WILL wake up at 5:00   once a year.
DID/PAST   He played soccer            every day.
NEVER:  "three times to the week"  ·  "every days"  ·  "I twice a week go"
NEVER:  "two times a week"  ->  "TWICE a week" (natural)
```
