## BLOQUE 2 (85') — M15 Present Perfect con verbos regulares

- **Tablero verbatim (25', Guia 1 p.134).** Escribe y explica en este orden:

```
have / has  +  VERBO en participio (-ed)
Yo he jugado hockey.        ->  I have played hockey.
he / she / it  ->  HAS          (todos los demas: HAVE)
I have (not) played.   Have I played?      I've played  / I haven't played
He has (not) played.   Has he played?      He's played  / He hasn't played
They have (not) played. Have they played?  They've played / They haven't played
Short answer: "Have you visited London?"  "Yes, I have." / "No, I haven't."
```

  Regla 1 del libro tal cual: *"Al contrario del pasado simple, usamos el presente perfecto para acciones realizadas en un periodo indefinido o acciones no terminadas."* Contrasta con la columna de ejemplos p.135: *I played soccer yesterday → I've played soccer · Daniela studied last night → Daniela has studied · I didn't talk to Miguel → I haven't talked to Miguel · Did you study last week? → Have you studied?* 6-8 pares, coro + individual.
- **Historia interactiva (25'): "the coworker who has already done everything".** Narras 2-3 frases con present perfect regular (*"It's 7:00 in the morning. He has arrived at the office. He has cleaned the desks. He has answered forty emails. He has called every client."*) → PAUSA → el grupo repite el fragmento → un estudiante AGREGA la siguiente cosa que ese companero de trabajo ya ha hecho (*"He has..."*) y TU la tejes en la historia. NUNCA huecos para llenar, nunca texto escrito antes. Cada 3-4 aportes conviertes una frase en pregunta (*"Has he cleaned the desks?" — "Yes, he has."*). Cierre: 2-3 estudiantes recuentan la historia completa de memoria.
- **Produccion con meta (35').** Meta en 1 linea, dila en ingles al abrir: *"By the end of this block, each of you will tell the group 3 things you have done this year and will ask 2 questions with 'Have you...?'"* Preparacion en parejas 8' (oral, notas minimas) → ejecucion uno por uno frente al grupo, el que termina le hace su pregunta al siguiente → feedback: primero si logro comunicar, DESPUES la forma. Corrige con *"No. Listen."* + version correcta + el estudiante repite 2x.

- **Drill de movimiento (25'), TODOS DE PIE, sillas atras.**
  - Ronda 1 — cadena de participios: tu dices el infinitivo (*"eat"*), el primero dice el pasado (*"ate"*), el segundo el participio (*"eaten"*), el tercero una frase completa (*"I have eaten arepa today."*). Sigue con *go, take, drive, wake up, tell, find, be*. Velocidad creciente.
  - Ronda 2 — "si es correcta, cambias de puesto": tu das una frase en pasado simple (*"You took a bus to the school."*), el estudiante la pasa a present perfect (*"You have taken a bus to the school."*); si esta bien, cambia de lugar con alguien.
  - Ronda 3 — cadena de preguntas reales: *"Have you driven a car?" — "Yes, I have." / "No, I haven't."* y el que responde le pregunta a otro con OTRO verbo irregular. Cierre: 2 min de produccion libre de pie.

## ANEXO — M15 + M16 verbatim, Plan B y nota interna (no leer en clase)

**M15 — "I Have Visited London" — The Present Perfect (Regular Verbs), p.133-146. Objetivo del libro:** *"Expresar frases usando el presente de perfecto."*

**Guia 1 (p.134), reglas tal cual:** 1. *"Al contrario del pasado simple, usamos el presente perfecto para acciones realizadas en un periodo indefinido o acciones no terminadas. Note que, a pesar de su nombre, es un tiempo pasado."* · 2. *"Lo formamos con el verbo 'to have' (tener) como auxiliar (igual a 'haber' en espanol) y el participio pasado del verbo."* · 3. *"La mayoria de los participios (llamados los 'participios regulares') se forman agregando '-ed' al verbo, igual al pasado simple y parecido al '-ado' o '-ido' de espanol."* · 4. *"Se usa 'have' como auxiliar normal, (recuerde: 'he', 'she' y 'it' usan la forma 'has')."* · 5. Version abreviada: *I've / You've / He's / She's / It's / We've / They've played* — negativa *haven't / hasn't played.* · 6. Respuesta corta: *"Have you visited London?" "Yes, I have." / "No, I haven't."* — *"Has Valeria visited London?" "Yes, she has." / "No, she hasn't."*

**Ejemplos (p.135), pasado simple → presente perfecto:** *I played soccer yesterday → I've played soccer · You lived in Madrid last year → You've lived in Madrid · Jeff showered in the morning → Jeff has showered · The dog arrived at 12:30 → The dog has arrived · We visited my parents last month → We've visited my parents · I didn't talk to Miguel → I haven't talked to Miguel · Arnold didn't want ice cream → Arnold hasn't wanted ice cream · Did Cristian arrive at 7:00? → Has Cristian arrived? · Did we need a car on Wednesday? → Have we needed a car?*

**Practicas verbales M15 (encabezados, p.137-146):** *VP1 Affirmative (45 seg) · VP2 Negative (45 seg) · VP3 Interrogative (45 seg) · VP4 Mixed (45 seg) · VP5 Short Answer (60 seg).* Ejemplos de respuesta: *He's played badminton · You've worked in the office · They've visited John · Has Luz played golf? — No, she hasn't · Have Mary and Fred liked Orlando? — No, they haven't.*

**M16 — "I Have Gone to Paris" — The Present Perfect Irregular, p.147-162. Objetivo del libro:** *"Expresar frases usando el presente de perfecto con verbos irregulares."*

**Vocabulario (p.148):** *be (ser/estar) am-is-are / was-were / been · take (tomar un bus) took / taken · drive (manejar, conducir) drove / driven · wake up (despertarse) woke up / woken up · eat (comer) ate / eaten · go (ir) went / gone · tell (contar, informar) told / told · find (encontrar) found / found.* La columna de fonetica va en blanco: *"Write the word as you hear it."*

**Guia 1 (p.149):** 1. *"Aunque muchos verbos (regulares) forman su forma participio agregando '-d' o '-ed', hay un grupo de verbos 'irregulares' que forman el participio en una manera impredecible."* · 2. *"Aunque la unica manera de aprenderlos es por memoria, solo hay alrededor de 30 en uso comun."* · 3. *"Se usa 'have' como auxiliar normal, (recuerde: 'he', 'she' y 'it' usan la forma 'has')."* Modelo: *Yo he comido pizza → I have eaten pizza.*

**Ejemplos (p.150), 3 columnas presente / pasado / presente perfecto:** *I am in Madrid → I was in Madrid → I have been in Madrid · You take a bus to the school → took → have taken · Sam drives a Suzuki → drove → has driven · Daniela wakes up at night → woke up → has woken up · The dog eats meat → ate → has eaten · We go to Bogota in our car → went → have gone · The children tell their parents → told → have told · I don't find my dog → didn't find → haven't found · Fred doesn't take a taxi to work → didn't take → hasn't taken.*
