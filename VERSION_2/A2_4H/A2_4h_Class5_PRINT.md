## BLOQUE 2 (85') — M11 El segundo condicional

- **Tablero verbatim (M11, Guide 1, p.88) — 12':** escribelo tal cual y leelo una vez.
1. "El segundo condicional" se usa para eventos improbables, imposibles o imaginarios.
2. Describe no lo que *haremos*, sino lo que *hariamos* bajo circunstancias diferentes.
3. En espanol el verbo va en SUBJUNTIVO ("Si yo tuviera plata... compraria un carro"). En ingles el subjuntivo casi no existe: se usa el PASADO SIMPLE + "would".
4. "Would" ("wud") corresponde a -ia, -ias, -iamos, -ian. Negativo: "would not" / "wouldn't" ("wudunt").
5. En negativo e interrogativo se trata igual al primer condicional.

```
IF + PASADO SIMPLE        ,   WOULD + VERBO
If I had money            ,   I would buy a car.
(Si yo tuviera plata...        ...compraria un carro.)

NEG:  If they had no money, they wouldn't go to the store.
QUES: If you ate lunch, WHERE would you eat?
      If Jeremy was sick, WHO would he call?
```

- **Historia interactiva (35'): "THE EMPTY OFFICE".** Narras 2-3 frases y paras. *"Early morning. Nobody has the keys to the office. The secretary says: 'If I had the keys, I would open the door.' The engineer says: 'If I spoke English, I would call the boss in London.'"* → PAUSA → el grupo repite el fragmento en coro → **un estudiante AGREGA** el siguiente personaje del edificio con su condicional (*"The nurse says: 'If I had a car, I would...'"*) y TU lo tejes con la siguiente frase. Cierre: 2-3 estudiantes recuentan la historia completa y 1 hace la pregunta final.
- **Meta en 1 linea (38'):** *"By the end of this block, each of you will tell the group 3 real 'if' ideas — about money, about your job, about your city — and ask 2 questions with 'what/where/who would you...?'"* Preparacion en parejas (8', ORAL, maximo 5 palabras anotadas) → ejecucion uno por uno frente al grupo → feedback: primero si LOGRO comunicar la idea, despues la forma (*would* + verbo simple, sin *to*). ## BLOQUE 3 (90') — M12 Pasado simple vs. continuo + Simulacion

- **Tablero verbatim (M12, Guide 1, p.100) — 12':**
1. Pasado simple = un evento o accion simple, terminado en el pasado en un tiempo especifico. *"I played yesterday."*
2. Pasado continuo = una accion en el pasado que tenia una extension o duracion. *"I was playing for three hours."*
3. Uso clave de las dos juntas: describir una actividad corta o abrupta que ocurre o INTERRUMPE otra mas extensa. *"I was playing when my mom called."*

```
LARGA (was/were + ...ing)   +   WHEN   +   CORTA (pasado simple)
I was eating pasta          when       my brother arrived.
My dad was working          when       I ate lunch.
NEG:  I wasn't driving the Model T last year.
QUES: What were you doing when I was studying?
      Where was Joe working last year?
```

- **Drill de movimiento (33'), TODOS DE PIE:**
  - Ronda 1 — "cut the long action": tu dices una accion larga (*"I was cleaning the house."*) y la cadena le agrega la interrupcion (*"...when the phone rang."*). Si esta bien, cambia de lugar en el circulo. Velocidad creciente.
  - Ronda 2 — cadena real: *"What were you doing at 8:00 last night?" — "I was cooking when my sister arrived."* — el que responde le pregunta al siguiente con otra hora.
  - Ronda 3 — mezcla con el Bloque 2: tu das el hecho pasado (*"I was working when the client called."*) y el estudiante lo vuelve hipotetico (*"If I was working, I wouldn't answer."*). Cierre: 2 min de produccion libre encadenada.
- **Simulacion (45'): THE INCIDENT REPORT — OFFICE FRONT DESK.** Escenario profesional montado solo con tablero y sala: en una oficina desaparecio un paquete del escritorio de recepcion entre las 3:00 y las 4:00. La coordinadora abre el reporte y entrevista a quien estaba en el piso. En el tablero escribes ANTES: *What were you doing at 3:00? · Who was working with you? · What happened when the courier arrived? · If we had cameras, what would we know?*
  - Al frente: **1 estudiante es el TESTIGO (guest)** + 1 estudiante es el/la COORDINADOR/A que toma el reporte. El coordinador pregunta; el testigo responde con accion larga + interrupcion (*"I was printing the report when the courier arrived."*).
  - El RESTO son OBSERVERS ACTIVOS, cada uno con papel: anotar **2 errores** (sobre todo *was/were* omitido o *-ing* faltante), **1 acierto** y **1 pregunta** para el debrief.
  - TU eres COACH desde afuera: NUNCA juegas testigo ni coordinador. Si se congelan 15+ seg → UN prompt corto (*"What were you doing?"*) y te retiras.
  - Minimos por ronda: 4 preguntas *"What were you doing when...?"* + 4 respuestas con interrupcion + 1 cierre hipotetico con *would* (*"If I saw the man, I would tell you."*).
  - Rotacion cada 5-6 min (3-4 rondas). Debrief (8'): observers reportan en anonimo; correccion clave al tablero — *"I printing when he arrived."* → *"I WAS printING when he arrived."*, coro 2x.
  - **MY YEAR — pieza 5 (dentro del debrief, SIN tiempo extra):** al cerrar el debrief di *"This is your MY YEAR piece 5: If I had more time at work, I would..."* y cada estudiante remata con 2-4 frases propias en segundo condicional sobre su trabajo o su semana real. Esa pieza es el audio de hoy.

## ANEXO — M11 y M12 verbatim + Plan B (no leer en clase)

**Libro A2, Module 11 — "I'd Eat a Cookie. The Second Conditional" (p.87-98). Objetivo del libro:** "Usar correctamente el segundo condicional en frases afirmativas, negativas, e interrogativas."

**Guide 1 (p.88), reglas tal cual:** 1. "El segundo condicional" se usa para eventos improbables, imposibles o imaginarios. 2. En otras palabras, se usa para describir no lo que *haremos*, pero lo que *hariamos* bajo circunstancias diferentes. 3. "Si yo tuviera plata... compraria un carro" implica que en realidad no tengo plata y por tal razon no comprare un carro. 4. Como el subjuntivo ya casi esta obsoleto en ingles, se usa el pasado simple en su lugar: *"If I had money… I would buy a car."* 5. El auxiliar "would" ("wud") corresponde a las terminaciones -ia, -ias, -iamos, -ian. 6. Su forma negativa es "would not" o "wouldn't" (pronunciada "wudunt"). 7. En sus formas negativa e interrogativa, se trata el segundo condicional igual al primero.

**Ejemplos del libro (p.89):** *If I played, I would play soccer. · If we traveled, we would fly to Paris. · If you ate lunch, where would you eat? · If I woke up late, I wouldn't shower in the morning. · If you worked in London, who would you live with? · If we didn't watch the game, what would we do? · If I had a car, I would drive to Bogota. · If the girls were hungry, where would they eat? · If Jeremy was sick, who would he call? · If they had no money, they wouldn't go to the store. · If you found your dog, you would be happy. · If you wanted a cookie, where would you buy it?*

**Practica Verbal (encabezados, p.91-98):** *VP1 The 2nd Conditional · VP2 Negative Expressions · VP3 Questions (what / who / where / what time) · Practica Verbal 4 (responder logicamente, max 60 seg).* Respuestas modelo del libro: *"If I had a car, I would drive." · "If I felt sick, I would call the doctor." · "If I won the game, I would feel great." · "If I was late, I would take a taxi."*

**Libro A2, Module 12 — "I Was Playing when My Mom Called. Past Simple vs. Past Continuous" (p.99-106). Objetivo del libro:** "Diferenciar correctamente entre los usos del pasado simple y el pasado continuo."

**Guide 1 (p.100), tal cual:** 1. Pasado simple = "un evento o accion simple, terminado en el pasado en un tiempo especifico" (*"I played yesterday."*); pasado continuo = "un evento o accion en el pasado que tenia una extension o duracion" (*"I was playing for three hours."*). 2. "Otro uso muy importante para las dos formas es describir una actividad corta o abrupta que ocurre o interrumpe otra actividad mas extensa": *"I was playing when my mom called."*

**Ejemplos del libro (p.101):** *I was living in Bucaramanga in 2019. · I ate a sandwich at 8:00. · Eliana was studying for her exams last week. · Where was Joe working last year? · I wasn't driving the Model T last year. · I was eating pasta when my brother arrived. · My dad was working when I ate lunch. · What were you doing when I was studying? · We were walking in the park, when Jeff saw a dog. · The girls weren't driving to school when their dad took a cab. · The family was watching a movie when Sebastian left.*

**Respuestas modelo de Practica Verbal (p.104, p.106):** *"The boy felt sick while the woman was swimming." · "The children ate breakfast while the man was working." · "We were visiting the maid when the pilot went to the office." · "The girl was walking when the group bought shoes." · "The student was watching a movie when I went to bed."*
