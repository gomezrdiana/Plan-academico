## BLOQUE 2 (85') — M13 Already, Still, Until

- **Tablero verbatim (M13, Guide 1, p.108) — 15':** escribelo tal cual y leelo una vez.
1. "Already" = "ya". Indica que la accion ocurrio o empezo antes del presente (u otro punto de referencia). Es un adverbio de frecuencia: va ANTES del verbo principal (o DESPUES de "to be"). *"I already studied."* / *"I was already in Paris in 2019."*
2. "Still" = "todavia". Indica que la accion esta ocurriendo todavia en el presente o punto de referencia. Se posiciona igual que "already". *"I am still in Paris."*
3. "Until" = "hasta". Indica que la accion se para en el punto especificado. Se coloca igual al espanol. *"I was in Paris until 2019."*
4. Los tres se pueden usar en pasado, presente y futuro, y en sus versiones continuas.

```
LINEA DE TIEMPO (dibujala):  2018 --- 2019 --- *AHORA* --- 2021 --- 2022
ALREADY -> ya paso / ya empezo antes del punto
STILL   -> sigue pasando EN el punto
UNTIL   -> se detiene EN el punto

I ALREADY studied.        The girls ALREADY ate lunch.
I am STILL in Paris.      Karol STILL wants a cookie.
I lived in Canada UNTIL 2012.   I can help you UNTIL 3:00.
QUES: Are you STILL in class? · Until when will you live in Colombia?
```

- **Historia interactiva (35'): "THE STORE THAT OPENED LATE".** Narras 2-3 frases y paras. *"At 8:00 the manager already opened the store, but the cashier was still counting the money. The delivery man waited outside until 9:30. At 9:00 the first customer already wanted a cookie…"* → PAUSA → el grupo repite el fragmento en coro → **un estudiante AGREGA** el siguiente personaje del local con SU marcador (*"The cleaner was still…" / "The boss already…"*) y TU lo tejes con la frase siguiente. Cada 3-4 aportes lanzas una pregunta a otro estudiante (*"Was the cashier still counting at 10:00?" / "Until what time did the delivery man wait?"*). NUNCA huecos para llenar. Cierre: 2-3 estudiantes recuentan la historia entera con los 3 marcadores.
- **Meta en 1 linea (35'):** *"By the end of this block, each of you will describe your real day with the three markers: one thing you ALREADY did, one thing you are STILL doing, and one thing you will do UNTIL a specific hour."* Preparacion en parejas (8', ORAL, maximo 5 palabras anotadas) → ejecucion uno por uno frente al grupo → feedback: primero si LOGRO comunicar, despues la posicion del marcador (*already/still* antes del verbo principal, despues de *to be*). Correcciones al tablero, anonimas.

## BLOQUE 3 (90') — M14 While, During, Again + Simulacion

- **Tablero verbatim (M14, Guide 1, p.122) — 12':**
1. "While" = "mientras", exactamente como el espanol. Conecta DOS FRASES COMPLETAS. *"I studied while you worked."* / *"I was studying while you were working."*
2. "During" = "durante", exactamente como el espanol. Conecta una frase con UN EVENTO O PERIODO. *"I studied during the party."* / *"I won't study during the night."*
3. "Again" = "otra vez / de nuevo". Generalmente al FINAL de la frase, aunque puede ir antes o despues de una frase de tiempo. *"I ate at 1:00. I ate again at 2:00. I will eat again at 4:00."*

```
WHILE  + FRASE COMPLETA   ->  I was helping my mom WHILE you visited your friends.
DURING + EVENTO/PERIODO   ->  The children swam DURING the morning.
AGAIN  (al final)         ->  My dad met the doctor AGAIN yesterday.
OJO (nota del libro p.127): en futuro, despues de WHILE va PRESENTE, no futuro.
     SI: "We will eat breakfast while the children TAKE a taxi."
     NO: "...while the children WILL TAKE a taxi."
```

- **Drill de movimiento (33'), TODOS DE PIE:**
  - Ronda 1 — "while o during": tu dices el complemento (*"…the class" / "…you were sleeping" / "…the morning" / "…I studied"*) y la cadena arma la frase completa eligiendo el conector correcto. Si acierta, cambia de lugar en el circulo. Velocidad creciente.
  - Ronda 2 — cadena de *again*: cada estudiante dice algo que hizo hoy y el siguiente lo repite en futuro con *again* (*"I drank coffee at 7:00." — "I will drink coffee again at 11:00."*).
  - Ronda 3 — mezcla con Bloque 2: tu das el hecho y la cadena lo dice con los 5 marcadores en fila (*"He already worked… he was still working at 4:00… he worked until 6:00… he worked while I studied… he will work again tomorrow."*). Cierre: 2 min de produccion libre.
- **Simulacion (45'): THE SHIFT HANDOVER — HOTEL FRONT DESK.** Escenario profesional montado solo con tablero y sala: cambio de turno en la recepcion de un hotel. Quien sale entrega el reporte de lo que paso; quien entra pregunta. En el tablero escribes ANTES: *What happened during your shift? · Is room 402 still waiting? · Did the group already check in? · Until what time is the restaurant open? · Did the guest call again?*
  - Al frente: **1 estudiante es el RECEPCIONISTA QUE SALE (guest)** + 1 estudiante es quien ENTRA al turno y pregunta. El que sale responde usando los 5 marcadores (*"The group already checked in. Room 402 is still waiting for towels. I was answering the phone while the manager talked to the guest. Two guests called again during the afternoon."*).
  - El RESTO son OBSERVERS ACTIVOS, cada uno con papel: anotar **2 errores** (sobre todo *during* usado con frase completa, o *already* despues del verbo), **1 acierto** y **1 pregunta** para el debrief.
  - TU eres COACH desde afuera: NUNCA juegas recepcionista. Si se congelan 15+ seg → UN prompt corto (*"And during the afternoon?"*) y te retiras.
  - Minimos por ronda: 2 frases con *already* + 2 con *still* + 1 con *until* + 2 con *while/during* + 1 con *again*.
  - Rotacion cada 5-6 min (3-4 rondas). Debrief (8'): observers reportan en anonimo; correccion clave al tablero — *"During I was working…"* → *"WHILE I was working…"* (during + evento, while + frase), coro 2x.
  - **MY YEAR — pieza 6 (dentro del debrief, SIN tiempo extra):** al cerrar el debrief di *"This is your MY YEAR piece 6: What I already finished, what I am still doing, and what I will do again this week."* y cada estudiante remata con 2-4 frases propias usando *already / still / again* sobre su semana real. Esa pieza es el audio de hoy.

## ANEXO — M13 y M14 verbatim + Plan B (no leer en clase)

**Libro A2, Module 13 — "I already knew that! Already, Still, and Until" (p.107-120). Objetivo del libro:** "Expresar frases usando los tres especificadores de tiempo de este modulo."

**Guide 1 (p.108), reglas tal cual** *(el libro las numera 3-6):* 3. "Already" → "ya". Indica que la accion ocurrio o empezo antes del presente (u otro tiempo de referencia). Es un adverbio de frecuencia como "often". Lo colocamos antes del verbo principal (o despues de "to be"). E.g. *"I already studied."* / *"I was already in Paris in 2019."* · 4. "Still" → "todavia". Indica que la accion esta ocurriendo todavia en el presente o punto de referencia. Tambien es adverbio de frecuencia y se posiciona igual a "already". E.g. *"I am still in Paris."* · 5. "Until" → "hasta". Indica que la accion se para al punto especificado. Se coloca igual al espanol. E.g. *"I was in Paris until 2019."* · 6. En la mayoria de los casos se puede usar "already", "still" y "until" igualmente en el pasado, presente y futuro, mas sus versiones continuas.

**Ejemplos del libro (p.109):** *Richard already visited his mother. · I lived in Canada until 2012. · I still love my friends. · Sebastian will study until March, 2022. · The girls already ate lunch. · Do you still like hamburgers? · The children swam until 12:00. · I won't go to bed until the morning. · Karol still wants a cookie. · Are you still in class? · We are still at the school, but we already finished the class. · I can help you until 3:00. · Does Fritz still hang out with Dale? · Beatriz is still finishing the project. · My friends were working until 7:30. · We will be playing until 8:30. · Until when will you live in Colombia?*

**Practica Verbal (encabezados, p.111-120):** *VP1 "Already" (punto de referencia: ahora) · VP2 "Already" (punto de referencia en el pasado) · VP3 "Still" (mixto) · VP4 "Until" (pasado o futuro) · VP5 los tres juntos.* Respuestas modelo del libro: *"We already showered." · "The maid is already getting dressed." · "We are still showering." · "The maid was still working on Tuesday." · "I will be living in Rome until twenty thirteen." · "We were showering until four o'clock."*

**Libro A2, Module 14 — "We Studied During the Game Again! While, During, and Again" (p.121-132). Objetivo del libro:** "Expresar frases usando los tres especificadores de tiempo de este modulo."

**Guide 1 (p.122), tal cual:** 1. "While" → "mientras". Exactamente como el espanol. Se usa para conectar dos frases completas. E.g. *"I studied while you worked."* / *"I was studying while you were working."* · 2. "During" → "durante". Exactamente como el espanol. Se usa para conectar una frase con un evento o periodo. E.g. *"I studied during the party."* / *"I won't study during the night."* · 3. "Again" → "Otra vez / de nuevo". Exactamente como el espanol. Generalmente colocado al fin de la frase, pero dependiendo del contexto se puede colocar antes o despues de una frase de tiempo. E.g. *"I ate at 1:00. I ate again at 2:00. I will eat again at 4:00."*

**Nota del libro (p.127, respuestas VP2):** "Igual al espanol, para usar 'while' en el futuro, usamos el presente en la segunda clausula, no el futuro." E.g. NO es correcto decir *"We will eat breakfast while the children will take a taxi."*

**Ejemplos del libro (p.123):** *Richard visited his mother while she ate lunch. · I lived in Canada during the war. · I am in the park again today. · The girls ate lunch during the afternoon. · Will you shower while I get dressed? · I won't clean the apartment while you are speaking on the phone. · Did Karol want a cookie during the class? · Are you working again today? · I was helping my mom while you visited your friends. · Wilson will be at the mall during the break. · My dad met the doctor again yesterday. · Beatriz was finishing the project while I was sleeping. · My friends were working again at 7:30. · When will you visit Canada again?*
