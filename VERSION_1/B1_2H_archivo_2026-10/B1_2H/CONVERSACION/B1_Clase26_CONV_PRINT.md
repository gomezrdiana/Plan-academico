## BLOQUE 2 — ROUTINES AND GOALS AROUND A MOMENT (40')

**Objective: everyone describes real work routines (before / after / without + -ing) AND real work goals (want to / love -ing), mixing both.**

1. **Avance de proyecto hoy: MY STORY, MY GOALS — pieza nueva: "what I always do before a key moment, and what I decided to start doing at work" (1').** Meta en una linea: *"By the end of this block you say two routines and two goals, clean."*
2. **Tablero — el molde, solo uso ORAL (3'); la regla en papel la sella Gramatica Cl 26:**
```
TWO SHAPES, ONE VOICE
"Before leaving the office, I check my inbox."   (prep + -ing)
"After finishing a call, I write two notes."     (prep + -ing)
"I want to lead a small project this year."       (want to)
"I love solving problems, but I stopped working late." (love/stop + -ing)
before/after/without + -ing · want/decide TO · love/start/stop -ING
```
3. **Modelas 45 seg (2'):** 1 rutina (prep + -ing) + 1 meta (want to) + 1 cosa que amas o dejaste (love / stop -ing). NO explicas.
4. **Preparacion en parejas (7').** Cada uno arma 2 rutinas reales (prep + -ing) y 2 metas reales (want to / love -ing), notas minimas.
5. **Ejecucion (14').** En parejas, A cuenta rutina + meta; B escucha con tarea (¿la accion tras la preposicion es -ing? ¿el primer verbo pide "to" o "-ing"?) y RE-CUENTA una de A (*"Before leaving, he checks... and he wants to lead..."*). Cambio de pareja a mitad.
6. **Producto verificable (9').** Vuelta completa: cada estudiante dice al grupo SU rutina mas importante + SU meta #1 (20 seg). Tu cuentas en el tablero cuantos moldes limpios de cada tipo salieron.
