## BLOQUE 2 — HOW MANY / HOW MUCH / HOW MUCH IS IT? + VIDEO DE HITO (45')

**Objective: everyone asks and answers about real work quantities and prices, fast, across the three functions — and records the milestone video.**

1. **Avance de proyecto hoy: MY STORY, MY GOALS — pieza "how much I've grown: I didn't have many words before; now I have a lot" (1').** Esta pieza es la que graban hoy.
2. **Tablero — el molde oral (3'); la regla en papel la sella Gramatica Cl 30:**
```
COUNTING & PRICING AT WORK
COUNTABLE:   "How many clients are there?" - "There are a few / a lot of."
UNCOUNTABLE: "How much coffee is there?"   - "There's a bit of / a lot of."
PRICE (be):  "How much is the printer?"  - "It's $95." / "They're $6 each."
PRICE (cost):"How much does it cost?"  - "It costs $95." / "They cost $6 each."
(how many = countable · how much = uncountable & price · each = per unit)
```
3. **Modelas 45 seg (2').** 2 cosas reales de tu trabajo: una contable (*"How many reports...?"*) y una incontable o precio (*"How much does the license cost?"*). NO explicas.
4. **Preparacion en parejas (5', notas minimas en su papel).** Cada uno arma 2 pares reales de su trabajo — una pregunta HOW MANY y una HOW MUCH / precio — y repasa en voz baja su pieza "how much I've grown" (sin escribirla completa).
