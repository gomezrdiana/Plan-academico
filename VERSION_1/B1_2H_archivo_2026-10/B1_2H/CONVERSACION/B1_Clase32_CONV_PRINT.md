## BLOQUE 2 — WHOSE IS IT? / TIRING vs TIRED (40')

**Objective: everyone claims ownership and names a real work feeling, fast, across both structures.**

1. **Avance de proyecto hoy: MY STORY, MY GOALS — pieza "THIS PROGRESS IS MINE" (1').** Una linea: *"This progress is mine: I earned it, and the journey was exciting, not exhausting."*
2. **Tablero — el molde oral (3'); la cita del libro la sella Gramatica Cl 32:**
```
OWNERSHIP & FEELINGS AT WORK
OWNERSHIP: "Whose laptop is this?" - "It's mine." / "It's hers."
           "Whose idea was better?" - "Mine was."
FEELINGS:  -ING = the thing causes it: "The report is tiring."
           -ED  = I feel it:           "I am tired."
"The meeting was boring; I was bored."  "The news is exciting; I'm excited."
```
3. **Modelas 45 seg (1').** Reclamas algo tuyo del trabajo (*"This project is mine"*) + un sentimiento real (*"The deadline was stressful, but the result is exciting"*).
4. **Preparacion en parejas (6').** 2 objetos / tareas reales (de quien es) y 2 situaciones reales (tiring o tired, interesting o interested).
5. **Ejecucion rapida (16').** A pregunta *"Whose ___ is this?"* y B responde con pronombre; luego B nombra una situacion y A reacciona con la forma correcta (-ing la cosa / -ed la persona). Cambio de pareja a mitad.
6. **Producto verificable (10').** Vuelta completa: UNA frase de posesion + UNA de sentimiento (20 seg). Cuentas moldes limpios (mine vs my / tiring vs tired) en el tablero.
7. **Feedback (3').** Logro primero, forma despues. Errores en 2+ parejas al tablero ANONIMOS, coro 2x. Nombres a tu cuaderno.
