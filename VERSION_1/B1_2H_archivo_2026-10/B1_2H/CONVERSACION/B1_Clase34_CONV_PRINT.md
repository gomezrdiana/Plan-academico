## ANEXO — B1 Book M1 + M2 verbatim + Plan B (para tu preparacion, no se lee en clase)

**M1 — Question Tags (Guia 1 p.3; Ejemplos p.4).** Cita p.3: *"The question tag uses the same auxiliary as the principal statement. If the statement is affirmative, the tag is negative... If the statement is negative, the tag is affirmative."* + *"I am very tall, aren't I? (Amn't does not exist, so we use aren't for I)."* Ejemplos p.4: *"You went to school, didn't you? / You didn't go to school, did you?"* · *"The boys are playing tennis, aren't they?"* · *"We can eat lunch, can't we?"*
**M2 — Either / Neither / Both.** p.9: *"We use 'either' to say 'o' or 'cualquier'. It indicates TWO options."* · p.13 (IMPORTANT): *"'Neither…nor' are never used with a negative 'not'."* — *"I can speak neither Spanish nor English."* · p.17: *"we do not use 'both' to say 'tambien'; we use 'also' or 'too'."* — *"We can speak both Spanish and English."*
