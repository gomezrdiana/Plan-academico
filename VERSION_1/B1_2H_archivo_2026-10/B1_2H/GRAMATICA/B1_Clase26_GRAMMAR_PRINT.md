## BLOQUE 2 — DOS FORMAS DEL GERUNDIO EN PAPEL (45') — DE PIE

**Objective: reactivate M21 and M22 fast, then everyone MIXES both shapes on paper about real work.**

1. **Activacion M21 (7') — tablero compacto, cita del libro p.197-200.** Coro 2x + cada estudiante 1 rutina real de trabajo en voz alta:
```
GERUND AFTER A PREPOSITION (M21, p.197-200)
Regla: verbo justo DESPUES de preposicion = -ing.
  before / after / without / about + accion-ING
  "I never eat before swimming."  "I never swim after eating."
  "I never speak without thinking."  "You should ask before using the car."
```
2. **Activacion M22 (7') — tablero compacto, cita del libro p.207-209.** Coro 2x + cada estudiante 1 meta real ("I want to..." / "I love ...-ing"):
```
DOUBLE VERB: "to" or "-ing" (M22, p.207-209)
Regla: el PRIMER verbo decide la forma del segundo.
  TO (aun NO empezado):  want/need/decide/forget/have to
     "I want to play."  "We decide to travel tomorrow."
  -ING (YA empezado):    start/stop/finish/like/love
     "I love eating."  "The girls stop fighting."  "I start studying."
```
3. **Meta (1').** *"By the end of this block, this board holds 12 real work lines that MIX both shapes."*
4. **Preparacion en parejas, DE PIE junto al tablero (7').** Cada pareja arma 2 lineas que combinan ambos moldes (*"Before deciding to change teams, I always think."* / *"I love finishing a report without rushing."*).
5. **Ejecucion (12').** Cada pareja ESCRIBE su mejor linea mixta en el tablero, la dice, y el grupo verifica: ¿-ing tras la preposicion? ¿"to" o "-ing" correcto tras el primer verbo? Meta: 12+ lineas limpias.
6. **Errores tipicos al tablero (5'), coro 2x:**
   - "after eat lunch" -> "after eatING lunch" · "before to leave" -> "before leavING"
   - "I want learn" -> "I want TO learn" · "I love to solve" -> "I love solvING"
   - "I stopped to check" (= me detuve) -> "I stopped checkING" (= deje de)
7. **Producto verificable (6').** Lectura coral del tablero ("our 12 mixed lines"); feedback logro primero, forma despues.
