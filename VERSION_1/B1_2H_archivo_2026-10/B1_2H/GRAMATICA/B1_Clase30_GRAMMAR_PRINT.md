## BLOQUE 2 — HOW MANY / HOW MUCH / PRICES EN PAPEL (45') — DE PIE

**Objective: everyone builds counting, measuring and pricing sentences on paper — across the three modules, fast.**

1. **Tablero — cita del libro (8'):**
```
QUANTITIES & PRICES (A2 Book M32-M34, p.273-297)
M32 COUNTABLE (Guide p.275): "how many...?" = cuantos (contable)
   "How many dogs do you have?"  "How many dogs are there?"
   many / a lot of / lots of = muchos ; a few = unos (3-5)
M33 UNCOUNTABLE (Guide p.285): "how much...?" = cuanto (incontable)
   "How much milk is there?"   "I have a liter of milk / 300 pesos."
   a lot of / lots of = mucho ; a bit of = un poco de
M34 PRICE (Guide p.293-294): two ways ->
   be:   "How much is the car?" "It's $5." / "They're $5."
   cost: "How much does the car cost?" "It costs $5." / "They cost $5."
   each = per unit -> "The sandwiches cost $2 each."
```
   Coro 2x + cada estudiante 1 pregunta real por modulo.
2. **Meta (1 linea):** *"By the end of this block, the board holds 12 real work lines — 4 how many, 4 how much, 4 prices (with 'each' at least twice)."*
3. **Preparacion en parejas (6', de pie junto al tablero):** cada pareja arma 1 pregunta + respuesta con "how many", 1 con "how much" y 1 de precio (be o cost), reales de su trabajo.
4. **Ejecucion (12'):** cada pareja ESCRIBE sus 3 lineas en su zona del tablero, las dice, y el grupo verifica: ¿how many con contable? ¿how much con incontable o precio? ¿"costs" con -s en tercera persona? Meta: 12+ limpias.
5. **Errores tipicos (4'), coro 2x:** "how much friends" -> "how MANY friends" · "how many money" -> "how MUCH money" · "How much cost it?" -> "How much DOES it cost?" · "It cost $5" -> "It costS $5" / "They cost $5 each".
