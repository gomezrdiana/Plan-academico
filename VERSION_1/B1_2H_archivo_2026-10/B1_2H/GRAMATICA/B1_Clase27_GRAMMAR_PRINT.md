NON-SEPARABLE PHRASALS (M23, p.221-224)

Ej. libro: "Did you turn on the TV? — Yes, I turned it on" · "Will you turn off your phone? — Yes, I will turn it off" · "Did you put on your hat? — Yes, I put it on" · "Will Alvaro pick up his sister? — Yes, he will pick her up" · "Did you look up Shakira on Google? — Yes, I looked her up."
