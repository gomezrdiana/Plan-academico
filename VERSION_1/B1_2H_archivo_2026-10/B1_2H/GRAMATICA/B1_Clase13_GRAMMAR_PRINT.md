## BLOQUE 2 — DID Y DIDN'T EN PAPEL (45') — DE PIE

**Objective: everyone writes the negative, the yes / no question with its short answer and the WH question with DID and the bare verb — and knows when WHO goes without DID.**

1. **Tablero — la tabla (5'), DE PIE.** La clase es 100% ingles; las citas verbatim en espanol las tienes en el ANEXO.
```
ASK AND DENY THE PAST  (A1 Book M10, Guide 1 p.125 · M11 p.143 · A2 M12 p.101)
"DID has the same special property as DO / DOES: in the positive we usually
 drop it and move -d / -ed to the verb — NEVER in the negative or the question."
(-)  subject + DIDN'T + BASE      "Juan did not play soccer."  "I didn't go."
(?)  DID + subject + BASE?        "Did Juan play soccer?"
     SHORT ANSWER:                "Yes, they did." / "No, they didn't."
WH?  WH + DID + subject + BASE    "Where did Mafe work?"  "What time did you get dressed?"
IRREGULARS: never in the negative or the question -> "Did you GO?" "I didn't EAT."
WHO / WHAT = THE DOER -> NO DID:  "WHO called?"  "WHAT happened?"
     (WHO = the receiver -> DID:  "Who did Jefferson help?")
```
2. **Señala la logica (2'), DE PIE.** *"DID already carries the past. If the verb carries it too, it is the past twice. And when WHO is the person who did it, the past goes back to the verb: 'Who called?'"*
3. **Drill en circulo, DE PIE (9').** Cadena cerrada. Tu das una afirmativa ("The client paid the invoice"). Estudiante 1 la niega; estudiante 2 la vuelve pregunta yes/no **y** da la short answer; estudiante 3 la vuelve WH (*"When did the client pay?"*); estudiante 4 hace la pregunta de sujeto (*"Who paid the invoice?"*) y lanza la siguiente afirmativa. Mete un irregular cada dos turnos. Velocidad creciente; la cadena no se detiene cuando alguien se cae. Segunda vuelta: cada estudiante cambia de rol en la cadena.
4. **Errores tipicos al tablero (4'), coro 2x cada correccion:**
   - "Did you worked?" -> "Did you WORK?"
   - "She didn't went" -> "She didn't GO" (irregular de vuelta a su base)
   - "Where did she went?" -> "Where did she GO?"
   - "Yes, I worked yesterday" (short answer larga) -> "Yes, I DID."
   - "Who did call the client?" -> "WHO CALLED the client?"
5. **Transform walk, DE PIE, en parejas (8').** Tablero en 4 zonas: `NEGATIVE | YES/NO + SHORT | WH | WHO / WHAT`. Dictas una afirmativa; cada pareja **CAMINA** a la zona que tu señalas **Y** escribe la transformacion en el tablero **Y** la dice en voz alta. La pareja que se equivoca la corrige la pareja de la zona vecina, con la formula de la semana: *"Almost — it's..."*. No tu.
6. **Ejemplos del libro, p.126 y A2 p.101 — EN EL PAPEL DEL ESTUDIANTE (13'), DE PIE junto al tablero. Dictado, nada impreso.** Dictas la frase afirmativa; ellos escriben **(a) la negativa** y **(b) la pregunta yes/no con su short answer**. Clave en el ANEXO, no la pongas antes.
```
DICTATION — write (a) the negative, (b) the question + short answer:
 1. He played soccer.               5. He helped Jonathan.
 2. She worked in the office.       6. I ate a sandwich at 8:00.
 3. It arrived at 1:00.             7. Steve woke up at 6:30 today.
 4. I lived in Canada.              8. She studied at 5:30.
```
   Caminas mirando papeles y anotas errores literales en TU cuaderno. **Correccion oral en cadena (ultimos 4 min):** un estudiante distinto lee cada item. Los items **6 y 7 se corrigen seguidos**: "ate" y "woke up" vuelven a "eat" y "wake up" — ahi es donde mas se cae.
